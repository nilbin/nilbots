# BreachApprentice author and qualification DX

## Scope and budget

- Role: `boundary-instrument`
- Target: cumulative T4
- Doctrine: objective initiative before conservative evasion
- Derived from: `arc-apprentice-t3-v1`
- Strategy changes: one contract-driven positional helper and one
  priority-order change
- Balance improvement passes: zero

## What worked

- The cumulative command automatically reran and hash-linked the complete T3
  prerequisite, so the positional policy could not skip lower-tier mechanics.
- Public observations exposed everything required by the initiative rule:
  active objective cells, projectile paths, projectile cadence, map collision,
  and legal moves.
- One symmetric policy crossed the pressure lane from both assignments without
  probe IDs, map IDs, fixed coordinates, or hidden engine state.
- The platform-matched ARM64 Docker builder produced the same WASM bytes in
  roughly seven seconds.

## Friction and findings

- ArcApprentice's generic two-advance dodge was locally safe but strategically
  over-conservative: in one mirrored assignment it repeatedly retreated from
  the choke and never entered the objective.
- Merely reducing the global dodge horizon would blur the T3 safety behavior.
  The added rule is intentionally narrower: it applies only before objective
  entry, only under a visible near-term threat, and only when the advancing
  tile remains safe through the next projectile advance.
- Passing suite 5 makes this artifact eligible as an entrant in a correctly
  gated balance study. It does not satisfy population breadth, lineage
  independence, paired-study, or entertainment-review requirements.
- Full cumulative evidence is about 195 MB for 36 self-contained replay files.
  The local bytes and tracked content manifest exist, but durable
  artifact-store upload is still required before release use.

## Population interpretation

BreachApprentice demonstrates that a small, general positional commitment can
separate T3 from T4. It remains the third revision of one authoring lineage.
Counting House, Arc, and Breach as three doctrines would overstate population
diversity; the next pilot work must author independently briefed T4+ policies.
