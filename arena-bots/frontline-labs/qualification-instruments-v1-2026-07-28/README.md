# Frontline qualification instruments v1

This cohort begins the current source-retained calibration and potential
system-opponent population. It is not a tournament and has no champion.

`house-apprentice` is the generated generic-actor starter after one mechanical
competency repair: after a projectile dodge, a body remembers its vacated tile
for one further tick so objective pathing cannot immediately walk it back into
the same shot. It passes the complete cumulative T2 profile. Its T3 boundary
is now measured: it retains T2 and fails the positive bend and cooldown-window
components.

`arc-apprentice` derives from that exact source/artifact lineage and adds
strict contract-driven curve previews plus objective-first routine tempo. It
passes the cumulative T2 prerequisite and complete T3 profile. Its T4 boundary
is now measured: it retains T3 and fails only the mirrored current-map entry
component.

`breach-apprentice` derives from ArcApprentice and adds one contract-driven
positional rule: when a move improves active-objective distance and is safe
through the next projectile advance, it takes that initiative before the
generic two-advance dodge window. It passes cumulative T4. This adjacent
revision is one boundary instrument and potential launch opponent, not an
independent doctrine.

Every body life receives a fresh bot instance, so retained memory is local to
one life. Even the T4 revision remains intentionally narrow: no opponent
model, curve mixture, forced-shot construction, transformations, body roles,
team tactics, or long-horizon commitment.

The full HouseApprentice 84 MB qualification replay tree is retained locally at
`evidence/house-apprentice-t2-v1/` and intentionally ignored by Git. The
tracked `evidence-manifest.json` preserves every relative path and byte hash.
Its cumulative T3 upper-boundary run is retained at
`evidence/house-apprentice-t3-boundary-v1/` with
`house-t3-boundary-evidence-manifest.json`. ArcApprentice's 141 MB cumulative
tree and hashes are retained in parallel at
`evidence/arc-apprentice-t3-v1/` and `arc-evidence-manifest.json`. Before this
cohort is used as durable release evidence, all qualification and boundary
trees must be copied to the project artifact store under their
content-addressed manifests. The suite-5 Arc boundary and Breach qualification
trees are retained at `evidence/arc-apprentice-t4-boundary-v1/` and
`evidence/breach-apprentice-t4-v1/`; their tracked manifests are
`arc-t4-boundary-evidence-manifest.json` and
`breach-evidence-manifest.json`.
