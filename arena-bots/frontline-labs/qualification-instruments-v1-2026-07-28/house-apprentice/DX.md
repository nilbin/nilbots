# HouseApprentice author and qualification DX

## Scope and budget

- Role: `boundary-instrument`
- Target: cumulative T2
- Doctrine: contract-driven apprentice baseline
- Origin: generated `generic-actor` starter, not an independently authored
  verdict-band doctrine
- Strategy changes: one mechanical competency repair
- Balance improvement passes: zero

## What worked

- The current scaffold exposed every T2 verb in small helpers and made the
  missing behavior easy to localize.
- The union profile caught old artifacts that assumed participant IDs were
  team IDs.
- Both automatic and explicit companion activation are covered.
- The controlled WASM artifact qualified in roughly five seconds after it had
  been compiled.

## Friction and findings

- The ordinary controlled Docker publish intermittently stalled after restore.
  Running the same pinned publish with `--disable-build-servers -m:1
  -p:UseSharedCompilation=false` completed in about 34 seconds. No toolchain
  source was changed here because the compiler investigation is separate.
- The first evade implementation sidestepped correctly, then objective pathing
  returned to the vacated lane one tick later. One tick of per-life
  dodge-origin memory fixed the mechanical contradiction.
- The draft qualification analyzer initially required a threat to be only one
  projectile advance away. A bot dodging two advances early then took no
  damage but could not pass. The profile was corrected before freezing so its
  declared two-advance hazard window and analyzer agree.
- Full qualification evidence is 84 MB for 14 short replay files because each
  replay is self-contained. The byte tree is retained locally and represented
  by a tracked content manifest, but still needs durable artifact-store upload
  before release use.

## Measured upper boundary

The cumulative T3 profile retains T2 and cleanly fails the positive-bend and
cooldown-window components from both assignments. It passes the paired
strict-corner, cadence, and local-form-safety components. This is now a useful
exact T2 calibration boundary, but it must not be relabelled T3 or used for
balance verdicts based on ordinary match performance.
