# Frontline Labs balance v3: Pressure bootstrap

This is a post-reveal, single-variable population-balance arm.

- Pressure changes one policy condition: when its only objective-capable body
  can legally Split, it does so before attacking or moving.
- Adapter, Fabricator, and Bastion are copied byte-for-byte from baseline v2
  and remain controls.
- The Frontline Labs rules, map, playlist, contract fingerprints, and numeric
  parameters remain frozen.
- The sprint uses seed `104729` with mirrored assignments: 12 matches. Earlier
  v2 evidence showed that extra seeds repeated every ordered trajectory.
- Every source and WASM artifact remains archived, including the superseded v2
  Pressure bot.

The arm tests whether Pressure's 0-12 baseline was caused by a circular policy
prerequisite rather than a weak Split mechanic or map geometry. It is not a
numeric balance or ship decision.

Acceptance criteria:

- all 12 matches verify with zero faults;
- Pressure completes Split in both assignments against all three opponents;
- Pressure earns at least two points across its six games;
- at least two head-to-head pairings are not 0-2 sweeps;
- Pressure's MaxTicks, stalled, and looped counts do not exceed the equivalent
  six-game v2 seed-104729 slice.

If Split activates in all six cells but Pressure earns no more than one point
and remains swept by every opponent, the bootstrap hypothesis is falsified.
