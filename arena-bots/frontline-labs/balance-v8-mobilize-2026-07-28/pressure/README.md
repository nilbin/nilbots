# Pressure

Balance v8 preserves this strategy source and its SDK/Guest 0.10.3 WASM
artifact byte-for-byte as a rules-unaware Mobilize compatibility sentinel.

> Seek early territorial advantage and credible breach opportunities without
> assuming escalation will unlock.

Pressure treats the live Frontline objective as the match's only durable
currency. Every mobile life independently derives the same deterministic
assignment from the current contract and allied observation: the closest
objective-capable body, with actor identity as the tie-breaker, takes or holds
the active position. Extra mobile bodies stage one position farther toward the
opposing base. If the enemy occupies or claims the live position, staging is
abandoned and pressure converges there.

Visible targets are engaged only along a clear, contract-ranged firing line.
The bot uses a legal aim-only shot program for diagonal mobile shots when the
current attack profile permits one, and absolute-heading fire when that
capability is present. It avoids visible projectile tiles while routing.

`split` is an opportunistic bonus when it is immediately legal and another
body can carry the objective assignment. The strategy never waits for Split,
Fabricate, Anchor, a particular unlock tick, or a fixed unit count. Turrets and
other zero-weight forms support with fire rather than pretending to capture.

All action codes and typed legal values come from the current tick's legality
catalog. Team advances, forms, objective weights, attack ranges, map geometry,
regions, and unit observations come from the resolved match contract.
