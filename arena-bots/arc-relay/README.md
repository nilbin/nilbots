# Arc Relay retained artifacts

Arc Relay Gate 3 freezes live here. Directories are append-only after their
manifest hashes are opened to evaluation.

- `stock-mind-v0/` — fixed stock execution engine plus one provisional
  evaluation sheet.
- `ambush-warren-11-stock-v1/` — immutable hosted playlist-v7 tactical mind
  bytes; its manifest pins the Warren-native Wellwright v1 sources.
- `ambush-warren-11-stock-v2/` — immutable hosted playlist-v8 repair built
  from the current tactical source through the controlled path, with a tracked
  build-input/artifact receipt. v1 remains untouched for historical v7 work.
- `ambush-warren-11-stock-v3/` — immutable hosted playlist-v9 direct-ladder
  mind and receipt. It runs the consolidated ATL1 sheet DSL; v1/v2 remain
  untouched for historical v7/v8 work.
- `ambush-warren-11-stock-v4/` — immutable hosted playlist-v10 tactical mind
  and receipt. It gives direct ladder intents semantic spectator names while
  preserving v9 gameplay behavior; v1/v2/v3 remain historical bytes.
- `ambush-warren-11-stock-v5/` — immutable hosted playlist-v11 tactical mind
  and receipt. It adds bounded recovery episodes, deterministic allied-traffic
  tickets, and the short oscillation circuit breaker; v1/v2/v3/v4 remain
  historical bytes.
- `ambush-warren-11-stock-v6/` — immutable tactical mind and receipt, minted
  for hosted playlist v12. It adds the team-relative Well
  subjects `own` and `enemy` so one sheet can name its own income on a map
  where each side owns a Well; movement, arbitration, and the ladder are
  unchanged from v5, and v1–v5 remain historical bytes.
- `ambush-warren-11-stock-v7/` — immutable hosted playlist-v13 tactical mind
  and receipt. It gives authored route traversal a persistent terrain course;
  because the registered stock-cell gameplay digest moves, v13 starts a fresh
  rating book and v1–v6 remain historical bytes.
- `ambush-warren-11-stock-v8/` — immutable hosted playlist-v14 tactical mind
  and receipt. It makes give-up reachability terrain-only, bounds custody
  fallback re-tests, and propagates loaded-carrier right-of-way through allied
  queues. The stock digest moves again, so v14 starts fresh and v1–v7 remain
  historical bytes.
- `ambush-warren-11-stock-v9/` — immutable hosted playlist-v15 tactical mind
  and receipt. It gives sticky authored courses bounded traffic detours that
  rejoin ahead without reselecting direction or lap, and makes authoritative
  actor movement wall-corner strict. The stock digest moves, so v15 starts
  fresh and v1–v8 remain historical bytes.
- `ambush-warren-11-stock-v10/` — immutable hosted playlist-v16 tactical mind
  and receipt. It stabilizes a carrier's once-per-tick delivery course, gives
  loaded carriers deterministic precedence by distance to their bank, and
  uses bounded launch-only reactor avoidance with an immediate normal-route
  fallback. The stock digest moves, so v16 starts fresh and v1–v9 remain
  historical bytes.
- `ambush-warren-11-stock-v11/` — immutable hosted playlist-v17 tactical mind
  and receipt. It remembers every observed loose Core through bounded fog,
  clears last-known objectives on conclusive evidence, and makes
  nearest-authorized friendly-drop recovery distance-first. The stock digest
  moves, so v17 starts fresh and v1–v10 remain historical bytes.
- `ambush-warren-11-stock-v12/` — immutable hosted playlist-v18 tactical mind
  and receipt. It keeps accidental-carrier drops claimable and prevents a
  loaded body from reserving a second Core while available couriers pass. The
  stock digest moves, so v18 starts fresh and v1–v11 remain historical bytes.
- `stock-mind-v3/` — unfrozen intelligent-gambit vertical-slice mind. It keeps
  the complete v2 baseline executor and reads provisional v2 operation data;
  it is evidence for an owner decision, not a launch artifact.
- `intelligent-gambit-v1-2026-08-02/` — baseline/on sheets and compact
  same-seed WASM records/broadcasts for Rear Hook and Lantern Sweep.
- `native-cohort-v1-2026-08-01/` — four independently authored native minds,
  their source/WASM revisions, sheets, DX notes, manifest, and durable match
  records/broadcast slices.

Canonical replays are gzip evaluation scratch only. They are verified and
deleted after scorecard extraction. A durable game contains only a match
record (at most 4 KiB) and a broadcast slice (at most 300 KiB gzip).
