# Arc Relay 32-sheet evaluation population

This archive retains the twelve 2026-08-01 evaluation controls and adds twenty
new sheets for the frozen Arc Relay stock mind. It is a sheet-space and gross
balance instrument, not a population of independent bot authors and not the
player-facing sheet format.

Each new sheet has a unique strategic thesis, composition, class-to-role and
theater allocation, route plan, policy set, and optional bounded rising-edge
gambit grammar. `static-distinctness.json` records the pre-outcome structural
gate. The gate weights composition, class/role/theater assignment, authored
routes, policy values, and gambit grammar; exact gameplay duplicates are
rejected independently.

The shared execution engine is the already-frozen
`../stock-mind-v0/bot.wasm`. It builds zero times for this expansion: every
sheet is validated, hashed, and delivered through the deterministic `ARS1`
evaluation envelope. Match records bind the frozen algorithm hash and each
sheet hash separately.

Regenerate and verify the pack with:

```bash
python3 scripts/generate-arc-relay-sheet-population.py
python3 scripts/generate-arc-relay-sheet-population.py --check
python3 scripts/test-generate-arc-relay-sheet-population.py
```

The twenty additions are:

- Smoke Convoy — two protected smoke-and-repair columns;
- Beacon Hunt — reveal, paint, and carrier collapse;
- Centre Phalanx — six-body constructed central lane;
- Outer Pincers — simultaneous outer race and displacement;
- Mine Crescent — outer denial around one centre runner;
- Pod Lattice — distributed three-lane construction;
- Rail Screen — two public firing corridors;
- Hook Burst — pull, paint, and radial carrier disruption;
- Null Veil — smoke-screened centre under suppression;
- Repair Web — three carriers inside overlapping sustain;
- Mortar Wheel — rotating courier/artillery groups;
- Counter Courier — safe outer lanes with reactive centre;
- Sensor Grid — reveal-fed mine and sentry control;
- Breach Column — concentrated centre break and south release;
- Elastic Reserve — three bounded role-switch reactions;
- Three-Well Race — four assigned pickup runners;
- Home Counterpunch — six deep interceptors and one courier;
- Smoke and Mines — concealed trap conversion; and
- Rotating Bastions — planted outer structures feeding a mobile centre.
