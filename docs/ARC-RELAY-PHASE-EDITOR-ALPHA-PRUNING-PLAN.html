Arc Relay Phase Editor Alpha Pruning Plan

Status: owner-approved implementation handoff, 2026-08-20.

Start condition

Do not begin this work until the currently running Scan / verified-clear / zone-bounded-not-fighting / Hold-facing implementation has finished, committed, and left the shared checkout clean. Re-read the resulting implementation before changing it. This plan consumes that work; it must not independently recreate or overwrite it.

Follow AGENTS.md, CLAUDE.md, web/CLAUDE.md, and .claude/skills/sheet-tuning/SKILL.md. Work on main unless there is a real need for isolation. Do not touch immutable historical playlists or tracked WASM artifacts for this editor iteration.

Goal

Make a useful phase straightforward to author from blank without exposing the full Tactics DSL. Keep the strategic depth in the compiler and executor, but reduce the normal phase editor to a small, truthful vocabulary.

The player should mostly answer four questions:

  1. Where should this group move or stand?
  2. How should it behave there and on the way?
  3. How should it recover?
  4. What observable event completes this phase?

This is a product-surface pruning pass plus the small semantic fixes required for the remaining controls to tell the truth. It is not a new executor, a second priority system, a balance pass, or a replacement for the existing Tactics coordinator and ordinary generated Instructions.

Locked product decisions

1. Visible phase job vocabulary

Offer only these four choices when authoring a new phase job:

  • Take up posts — assigned positions, one post per bot.
  • Converge on a spot — one shared destination.
  • Occupy an area — flexible positions inside a drawn area. Rename the current Hold an area; geometry must not also imply posture.
  • Patrol a loop — repeatedly follow a drawn loop.

Do not offer Line, Approach, Advance, Return, Escort, or Carry on as new job choices.

Carry-forward is still necessary, but it is implicit. When a new phase does not replace a group's ground, it inherits the prior effective job and geometry. The UI may explain that it is continuing the previous assignment, but the player must not select a fifth Carry on mode. It is acceptable to retain none as an internal encoding if that remains the cleanest materialization.

Keep legacy job parsing/materialization while it is cheap and harmless. A loaded draft that actively contains a hidden job must not silently lose it: show its current value as legacy content or normalize it deliberately with a clear explanation. Do not let built-in recipes create an apparently blank or uneditable job. Update recipes to the visible vocabulary where practical, while preserving the intent of Demolition March.

Destructive mode switching

Changing job type when compatible geometry already exists must show a confirmation before clearing anything. The message must name what will be removed, such as eight posts and their facings, an area, a route, or dependent spatial skill marks.

  • Default action: Cancel.
  • Confirm: clear only geometry that is incompatible with the new mode.
  • Offer Undo through the editor's normal document history/toast mechanism.
  • Do not warn when there is nothing material to lose.
  • Merely changing posture, travel behavior, recovery, or conditions must never clear geometry.

2. Visible destination posture

Offer only:

  • Observe — stay on the authored task and do not engage.
  • Hold — fight for the authored ground, turn toward threats from any direction, and do not meaningfully chase.
  • Aggressive — pursue and finish nearby enemies within the authored leash.

Fold Wait into Observe for normal authoring. Do not promise that Observe "breaks contact" unless evasive movement truly exists; the minimum honest behavior is no voluntary Fight instruction, continued movement toward/along the job, and retention or resumption of its ground.

Steal and Escort are Core-custody policies, not general destination postures. Hide them from this control. Existing internal values may remain where needed for old documents or generated custody behavior.

3. Visible on-the-way behavior

Offer only:

  • Just march — do not fight en route; the destination posture takes over on arrival.
  • Respond to contact — engage a nearby enemy with the short Hold leash, then resume the same route. This replaces the misleading label Answer attacks: the current mechanism reacts to nearby contact, not specifically to having received an attack.
  • Brawl through — use the Aggressive fight policy while travelling, then resume the route and hand over to the destination posture on arrival.

Hide Hang back. Its current rear-side destination bias is not enemy avoidance or safe-route planning, so do not rename it Avoid enemies. It can return in the future only with a separately approved, truthful mechanic.

Travel behavior is not a second order or priority layer. Continue compiling it as phase-scoped ordinary Instructions gated by whether the bot has arrived.

4. Visible recovery behavior

Offer only:

  • Fight on.
  • Break for the heal tile.
  • Heal up fully.

Hide Patch up close. Explain the actual health trigger and completion threshold in the option copy/readback; do not make a player infer them from the label. Recovery must still pre-empt the ordinary phase floor according to the existing Instruction priority and then return the bot to the same active phase.

Formation readiness

Rename Everyone in position to Formation is ready. Dead bots remain excused, but readiness needs one simple forgiveness control:

  • Everyone — zero late bots.
  • All but one — one late bot allowed.
  • All but two — two late bots allowed.

Default to Everyone for every job, preserving today's strict body count until the player deliberately allows late bots. Encode the setting explicitly, for example by allowing the current groupsSet: true to mean zero slack and adding an object form with allowedLate: 0|1|2. The exact wire shape is an implementation choice, but it must validate in both the TypeScript and C# models and survive a save/load/compile round trip.

Slack is team-wide body slack, not extra spatial radius. Do not change any job's current spatial tolerance and do not add a distance-tolerance control in this pass. In particular, an assigned Post currently counts as reached on its own tile or an adjacent tile (Chebyshev distance at most one); preserve that exact rule. Evaluate the allowed-late count only after applying the unchanged spatial checks.

Stable rejoin semantics

Freeze the readiness cohort when the phase begins:

  • A living bot at phase entry is required until it reaches its ground, dies, or the phase advances.
  • A bot dead at phase entry is excused for that phase.
  • Once a required life dies, its later respawn must not newly block readiness.
  • A respawned bot still receives and performs the active phase job.
  • A respawn may help occupy ground, but it must not increase the number of bodies the phase is waiting for.

Do not expose a rejoiner checkbox. This fixed rule prevents readiness from flapping because a respawn wave occurred while the team was assembling.

Exact Posts correctness

Posts retain stronger meaning than a bounding box:

  • Each squad slot is checked against its assigned post.
  • Use the existing per-post Chebyshev-distance-one tolerance unchanged.
  • One bot cannot satisfy two required posts, even when readiness ranges overlap.
  • Slack means an authored number of assigned posts may remain unfilled; it does not turn the formation into an undifferentiated area.
  • Apply this to large groups too. Remove the current shortcut where groups larger than three are accepted merely because every body is somewhere in the combined posts footprint. This applies the existing assigned-post check consistently; it must not tighten or expand that check's spatial radius.

If the ordinary condition-zone budget cannot express this honestly, perform the squad-level readiness calculation in the existing Tactics coordinator. Do not introduce another scheduler or reservation system.

Visible phase-completion conditions

The normal phase picker should contain only the following rows.

Our team

  • Formation is ready, including the slack control above.
  • Everyone is healed up.

Hide losses and losses-within from the normal phase surface.

Enemies

  • Any enemy contact.
  • At least N enemies were recently seen here. Rename Enemies gather somewhere. It uses bounded team knowledge, not omniscience. Stale knowledge must not satisfy it. Use one canonical fixed freshness window and state that window in helper/readback text rather than adding another ordinary control.
  • At least N enemies are currently down.
  • This area has been verified clear, using the accepted rolling-observation and Scan semantics from the preceding implementation.
  • No friendly is currently fighting in this area, using the accepted zone-bounded fight-state semantics from the preceding implementation.

Hide enemies destroyed this phase and enemies destroyed in a short window.

Multi-zone conditions

Every condition row that authors a zone must support between one and three zones. In the current phase surface this applies to recent-enemy presence, verified clear, and no-friendly-fighting; use the same reusable editor/model for any other zone condition that remains elsewhere in Tactics.

Multiple zones form one possibly disconnected watched area. They do not create another AND/OR editor:

  • Recent enemies: count distinct recently known enemy lives across the union. An enemy inside overlapping zones counts once.
  • Verified clear: every walkable tile in the union must satisfy the accepted rolling observation/no-unresolved-memory rule. Therefore every authored zone must be proved clear.
  • No friendly fighting: no qualifying friendly fight may intersect any tile in the union.

This gives the player a simple way to describe several entrances, both sides of a corridor, or disconnected pieces of tactically equivalent ground without duplicating an entire condition case. Per-zone enemy thresholds and arbitrary nested zone logic are out of scope.

The UI should show all zones in the condition's colour, distinguish them as Area 1–3, allow each to be selected/redrawn/deleted independently, offer Add another area until the cap is reached, and use an honest summary such as 3+ enemies recently seen across these areas or all three areas verified clear.

Use a one-to-three zones collection in newly authored documents. Continue to accept the existing singular zone shape as a one-element legacy form, and normalize it on an intentional edit/save rather than silently dropping it. Validate the cap and non-empty collection in both TypeScript and C#. Shared or overlapping tiles and actors must be canonicalized before evaluation so overlap never multiplies counts, scan work, or fight state.

Cores

  • A Core is up for grabs.
  • A Core spawns soon.
  • A Core was taken this phase.

Do not offer Core banked or Core lost as normal phase-completion conditions. Core custody is a temporary, higher-priority branch inside whichever phase is active:

  1. A bot acquires a Core.
  2. The active phase/tactic custody preset takes over: keep carrying, bank alone, or bank under escort.
  3. The Core is banked or lost.
  4. Custody ends and surviving bots resume the still-active phase.

A Core was taken this phase remains because an author may genuinely want the wider tactic to change after acquisition. Ordinary pickup, return, banking, loss, and resumption must not require a dedicated phase. Keep bank/loss facts internally if the custody executor needs them; merely remove them from normal phase authoring.

Time and ground

Hide all current rows:

  • We have been at this phase a while.
  • The destination is reached.
  • The ground has been watched for long enough.

Destination reached is not identical to Formation ready, but its current majority/first-mover meaning is weaker and harder to understand. Formation ready owns the common intent.

Ground watched long enough is not the inverse of clear. It currently means continuous observation even when enemies are present. Verified-clear plus Scan owns the common player intent. Keep the underlying fact only if it is still consumed elsewhere.

Hidden conditions and loaded drafts

Hide is not the same as silently deleting authored content. Continue parsing hidden condition rows unless removal materially simplifies the system. If a loaded draft actively uses one, show a compact Existing advanced condition readback with a way to remove it. Do not make hidden state invisible while it still changes execution.

Do not redesign the existing Required / Triggered / Unless and alternative-case semantics in this pass.

Strictly linear phase sequencing

Remove the next-phase picker and explicit authored phase jumps entirely.

  • Completing a non-final phase always activates the following phase in array order.
  • Completing the final phase finishes the tactic.
  • A phase with no completion cases remains active indefinitely.
  • Alternative completion cases all have the same destination.
  • A phase whose completion condition is already true may naturally advance on its normal first evaluation tick; do not add explicit skip pointers.
  • Tactic-wide abort/restart behavior is unchanged.

The persisted player-facing Tactics document should no longer require a to field on each transition. The compiler/coordinator may still generate or use an internal next-phase index/id as execution machinery, but it is not authored policy.

There are no meaningful saved explicit jumps that require a compatibility UI. Remove the picker, named-forward-target validation, summary wording, recipes, fixtures, and tests that exist solely to support arbitrary jumps. Do not build a migration subsystem for unused jumps.

Tactic completion and rearm

Remove the generated hidden 40-tick tactic rearm delay. It was inherited from the old root-Order cooldown; the current squad coordinator requires only one dark evaluation pass to clear per-body Instruction state between activations. There is no separate documented gameplay reason for the remaining 40 ticks.

Use these semantics when a tactic ends on tick T:

  • Its tags are dark for the completion evaluation, clearing the ended run’s Instruction episodes.
  • A different eligible tactic may still be selected immediately on tick T, following ordinary top-to-bottom tactic order.
  • If no other tactic becomes active, the same tactic may be selected again on tick T + 1 when its start conditions are still true.
  • A newly selected tactic remains non-pre-emptible under the existing coordinator rules; the completed higher tactic does not reclaim the team merely because it becomes eligible again.

Generate rearm: 0, or internalize the mandatory one-tick boundary in the coordinator if the generated field can be removed cleanly. Keeping the Orders program field for contract simplicity is acceptable; emitting 40 is not. Do not add a cooldown control to the simplified editor. If meaningful tactical cooldowns are wanted later, they require a separate owner decision and must be player-visible rather than hidden policy.

UI and authoring requirements

  • Keep the existing Nilbots visual language and map-first editor; this is not a mobile-first redesign.
  • From a blank sheet, the four job modes and their drawing affordances must be immediately understandable without reading DSL documentation.
  • The phase readback must use the same player-facing labels as the controls.
  • Hidden vocabulary must not leak through summaries, tooltips, validation errors, or blank recipe defaults during normal authoring.
  • Map geometry remains editable in place after the phase has been created.
  • No raw JSON editing may be required for any acceptance flow below.

Required tests

Document, compiler, and coordinator

  • Save/load/validate/compile each visible job, posture, travel behavior, recovery behavior, and condition.
  • Prove strict sequential phase activation and final-phase completion without authored targets.
  • Prove a phase with no completion condition holds indefinitely.
  • Prove multiple alternative cases all advance to the same next phase.
  • Prove tactic-wide abort behavior remains higher-level and unchanged.
  • Prove a tactic ending on tick T cannot reselect itself on T, may reselect on T+1 when no other tactic owns the team, and begins with fresh Instruction state.
  • Prove a different eligible tactic may begin on the completion tick and is not subsequently pre-empted by the completed tactic.
  • Prove Core custody bank and loss both return control to the same still-active phase unless that phase independently completed.
  • Prove A Core was taken this phase can still intentionally advance a phase.

Formation readiness

  • Zero, one, and two allowed-late settings.
  • Dead at phase entry is excused.
  • Death while assembling excuses that life.
  • Respawn during the same phase performs the job but does not re-block it.
  • Eight assigned Posts are checked individually.
  • Overlapping post tolerances cannot let one body satisfy multiple posts.
  • Area, spot, and patrol readiness use their honest job footprints.

Posture, travel, and recovery behavior

Use focused deterministic scene tests, not only materialized JSON assertions:

  • Observe reaches/retains its job and never emits a voluntary Fight action.
  • Hold turns and fires against frontal, side, and rear contact, respects its short leash, and returns to its ground.
  • Aggressive pursues within its leash and resumes its ground afterward.
  • Just march ignores contact en route and hands over on arrival.
  • Respond to contact fights with the short leash, resumes the same route, and does not keep the travel fight after arrival.
  • Brawl through uses the aggressive leash, resumes the route, and hands over on arrival.
  • Every recovery option pre-empts at its documented health threshold and then resumes the same phase/job.
  • Every emitted executor action carries a non-empty debugMessage explaining the selected phase intent.

Conditions and vision

  • Recent-enemy presence succeeds from fresh bounded team knowledge and fails once that knowledge is stale.
  • Every zone-bearing condition round-trips one, two, and three zones; zero and four are rejected in both validators.
  • Recent-enemy presence counts distinct enemy lives across the zone union and never double-counts overlap.
  • Verified clear cannot succeed from lack of vision; it requires the accepted rolling scan evidence and no unresolved enemy memory across every zone in the union.
  • No-friendly-fighting is affected only by fights intersecting its authored zone union.
  • Hidden active conditions in a loaded draft remain visible as legacy/advanced content until removed.

Frontend

  • Normal controls expose exactly the approved option sets.
  • A destructive job-mode switch can be cancelled with no document mutation.
  • Confirming the switch removes only incompatible geometry and can be undone.
  • A non-destructive switch produces no warning.
  • New phases carry prior ground implicitly.
  • No next-phase target picker appears.
  • Normal newly authored transitions serialize without arbitrary targets.
  • Zone-bearing rows can add, select, redraw, and remove up to three independently visible areas, and their summaries state the union semantics.
  • Built-in recipes open without invisible active jobs or conditions.

Required acceptance flow

Starting from a blank sheet in the visible editor, author a three-phase tactic without raw JSON:

  1. Take up assigned Posts under Observe. Continue when Formation is ready with one allowed late bot and at least N enemies recently seen in a drawn area.
  2. Occupy a drawn area under Hold, Respond to contact on the way, and Break for the heal tile. Continue when the drawn combat area is verified clear and no friendly is fighting there.
  3. Patrol a drawn loop under Aggressive/Brawl through and remain there because the final phase has no completion condition.

Give the tactic an ordinary Core-custody preset. In deterministic proof, a Core pickup must enter custody, bank or lose the Core, and return survivors to the same phase without a Core-specific phase or a bank/loss transition row.

The saved/read-back tactic must be understandable from the map and phase storyboard alone. Switching one populated job mode must visibly exercise the warning, Cancel, Confirm, and Undo flow.
