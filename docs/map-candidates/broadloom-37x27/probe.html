Broadloom probe settlement

96 deterministic matches on the real engine (Tapline-14 ruleset, Broadloom 37×27, branch agent/broadloom-probe). Settles the candidate report's open hypotheses. Probe-only — nothing is promoted. Doctrines share one composition and are authored twice: raw Orders sheets, then arc-relay-tactics-v2 documents (posts and patrol only) compiled through the real materializer — the player surface, end to end.

Motion audit of the decisive pairing: trade clean 12/12; the ball stalls in its own traffic (reactor-mouth carrier jams) in 6/12 — the known carrier-traffic executor seam. Clean-movement games: ball 4–2. The anti-ball margin is therefore an upper bound; the directional verdicts are not.

Settled

  1. The eight-body deathball has profitable, authorable counterplay. Custody posts + a posted mid line + a patrol raid — pure posts-and-patrol authoring — beats the mass 8–4, with 18–2 blowouts when the ball's congestion bites. On clean-movement games it is near parity: the mass is not dominant, and massing has a measured congestion cost.
  2. Raids are viable as motion, dead as siege. Same target, two authorings: a parked converge returned 7% (the respawn-adjacent quarter kills the return; every drop subsidizes the defender). A closed patrol loop over the roof with coreRule: hunt banked 85% (11:2) and simultaneously denied the defender's own drip. The roof flank is the raid artery in both directions, as drawn.
  3. Heal-home turtling is not viable. 0–24 vs the mass. The income floor is real (near-lossless drip, games stretched past t440) but conceding centre loses ~2:1 on charge, every time.
  4. The 8-step own-Well run is safe by default, deniable by commitment. Uncontested: near-lossless. A committed camp intercepted 20 carriers in one battery; the patrol machine cut the mass's own income 60%. The dial works.
  5. The workshop bias cannot confer ownership. 15 vs 18 steps is a ≤4-tick head start; both sides reach either workshop from centre in ~12.5t. Ownership would need a training channel under ~8 ticks — an order of magnitude below every existing channel. Recorded as a constraint on future workshop rules.
  6. The player surface authors Broadloom. All doctrines are tactics-v2 documents; the geometry gate caught four authoring mistakes (wall-facing posts, a post on a pillar) before any match ran.
