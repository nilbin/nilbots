## RESULT

Arc Relay's first native read and commander-depth audit are complete under the
corrected generic-mind engine `1.0.5`. The final evidence contains 68 match
records and 68 broadcast slices. All 68 scratch canonical replays were verified
and then deleted; none ships in either gallery or any durable match package.

Interception went 6-0, Split Control 4-2, Information/Route Control 1-1-4, and
Convoy 0-1-5. There were 10 distinct outcome signatures and 12 distinct replay
hashes in 12 matches. This is a useful first diagnosis, not a population
estimate: one campaign seed plus mirrors creates deterministic coverage cells,
not twelve independent observations.

layer is not falsified for lack of sensitivity. It did not validate the first
ordered-gambit grammar. Across all gambit-versus-static pairings, gambit sheets
went 6-1-25; within the same four families, only `convoy-safe--gambit` beat its
static counterpart. The top three totals were all static sheets. No strict
pair-majority three-cycle appeared.

The depth sheet schema is **provisional evaluation data only**. It was designed
for audit coverage, hashing, and reproduction. It is not the player-facing

The registered sharp question has a clear first answer: **gambit-bearing
sheets did not generally beat static sheets**.

- Across every gambit-versus-static pairing: gambit `6-1-25`.
- Within family: Balanced `0-0-2`, Convoy Safe `2-0-0`, Intercept Switch
  `0-0-2`, Split Fast `0-0-2` from the gambit side.
- The top three aggregate variants were static.
- No variant strictly pairwise-dominated every other variant. Split Fast
  static and Balanced static split their mirrors 1-1.
- There was no strict pair-majority three-cycle. Five pairings split their two
  assignments rather than forming a stable edge: Balanced gambit–Intercept
  gambit, Balanced static–Split Fast gambit, Balanced static–Split Fast static,
  Convoy gambit–Intercept gambit, and Convoy static–Split Fast gambit.
- Sheet sensitivity is high: totals range from 1 to 11 wins, 52/56 outcome
  signatures are distinct, and Convoy's ordered gambit reverses its own
  within-family result even though gambits lose overall.

The audit therefore does not falsify sheet authorship as inert, but it does
falsify the current ordered-gambit grammar as a generally beneficial adaptive
layer. This is evaluation-grade evidence about dimensions, not a proposed
product sheet shape.
