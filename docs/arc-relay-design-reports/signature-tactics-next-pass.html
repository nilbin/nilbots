Recommendation

Yes: run one more refinement round before adding more Tactics.

Its first task is combat lifecycle, not signature UI. The current map-first editor makes Posts the normal spatial assignment. Hold now correctly starts at its post, pursues within a bounded defence episode, then returns. Aggressive does not retain that episode after it takes the first step away from a post. That contradicts the editor promise “Chase, commit, finish kills.”

The corrected posts rule

Replace “authored posts always win” with this:

A post owns staging, resting facing, the fight's origin and the return destination. An active fight episode temporarily owns combat movement inside the posture's leash. Phase changes, Core custody, urgent recovery and signature commitment can pre-empt it normally.

It is not a new state machine.

Patrol remains different: its route is the moving ground. “On the way” behavior still governs ingress. The change is specifically that an arrived post does not pull an active Aggressive fighter home every second tick.

Patrol is moving ground—not a moving Post

Do not apply the station-gate repair to patrols.

A patrol has no arrival tile. Contact may start a fight anywhere on the route; that contact tile becomes the fight episode's fixed leash origin. When the interruption ends, the bot resumes its existing patrol cursor rather than restarting, reversing or pathing back to the first waypoint.

Combat

Interrupt, then resume

Hold or Aggressive temporarily owns movement inside its bounded fight leash. Observe continues the patrol without voluntarily engaging. Ending contact hands control back to the same route progress.

Skill marks

Use them in passing

Points and route segments become eligible only when naturally reached or within the skill's legal range. A bot must never reverse or abandon its loop merely to cast at a mark.

Failure

Never wait for a cast

If a marked use is unavailable, unsafe or illegal, the patrol continues that same tick and the opportunity may be reconsidered on a later lap.

Commitment

Pause honestly

An authoritative rooted tell or channel may hold the bot briefly. Completion or cancellation resumes the same patrol cursor; it does not manufacture a second route lifecycle.

Deployments

Revisiting is not replacing

A returning Minesmith, Mason or Nest does not replace a healthy useful deployment just because its patrol reaches that mark again.

Pre-emption

Normal priorities remain

Core custody, urgent recovery, phase change and abort still win. On return, the patrol resumes only if that phase and route are still active.

Patrol contact → latch ordinary fight at contact tile → pursue inside chaseLimit Fight ends → resume the same route cursor and direction Skill mark → use only while passing naturally; never divert or backtrack Skill declines → continue route in the same tick; retry only on a future legal pass Tell/channel → preserve authoritative commitment; then resume cursor Phase exits → discard the old patrol episode and cursor normally

Custody is a Core-bound phase overlay—not a replacement phase

Authoritative invariant: the Core remembers the phase that first took custody.

On the first friendly pickup, bind that stable CoreId to the active Tactic and pickup phase. That pickup phase supplies delivery plan, way home, escort Group and custody-safe skill policy for the Core's entire unresolved life. The squad may advance through later phases around it, but it must never silently reinterpret that Core using a later phase.

Current defect

The latch follows a body, not a Core

The current CustodyLatch is keyed by carrier unit and is deleted as soon as that body stops carrying. A pass binds the catcher afresh to its current phase; a drop followed by re-pickup does the same. Both violate the pickup-phase rule.

Required repair

Persist team-local custody provenance by CoreId

Extend the existing latch into a small provenance record keyed by team and stable Core identity. Carrier and escorts temporarily consume that record; changing carrier, dropping the Core or losing sight of it changes who consumes it, not which phase authored it. This is lifecycle memory for ordinary Instructions—not a second scheduler.

One skill plan

Signatures remain pickup-phase-local

The player's one bot/phase skill plan is reused under ordinary work and custody. While custody is unresolved, the pinned pickup phase supplies that plan even after the squad advances. Catalog metadata declares whether a use is carrier-safe, escort-useful or a custody special case. Relay Toss stays inside custody; Kestrel Dash is forbidden while carrying. No second set of “custody signatures” appears in the UI.

No recovery branch

Custody means stay on task

Do not add carrier-recovery toggles, escort thresholds or duplicated heal routes. Carrier and attached escorts ignore self-directed Recovery while custody is active. Nearby Patchbay support or passive healing remains legal when it does not abandon the task.

How the sixteen signatures enter the pass

Transit & custody · Kestrel, Relay
Kestrel: Dash only empty-handed along the active route/marked segment with ≥2 safe forward tiles. Never auto-Dash from a fixed post.

Relay: keep Toss inside custody; require useful forward progress and a legal receiver; failed Toss never prevents movement/banking.
Protect & sustain · Palisade, Patchbay
Palisade: screen a real hostile lane protecting self, carrier or ally; respect authored facing; do not replace a useful active screen.

Patchbay: prefer carrier/veteran/lowest health, require a plausible safe channel, then preserve the authoritative channel.
Control · Towline, Hush, Repulsor, Sunder
Towline: beneficial clear pull, carrier first; preview destination and interceptors.

Hush: suppress actual hostile signature value, not generic proximity.

Repulsor: preview every push and protect allied posts/Core direction.

Sunder: paint only when allies can plausibly spend the enhanced hits.
Ranged setup · Longshot, Mortar
Longshot: require the firing post/area, team contact and a clear line; preserve the rooted lock.

Mortar: require occupied/recently confirmed value and zero allies in the cross; render friendly fire and tell.
Information & concealment · Lantern, Veil
Lantern: for alpha, make Flare honestly anti-smoke; Smart fires only where smoke blocks a relevant allied sightline.

Veil: cover a carrier/crossing or break an enemy lane without cutting a critical friendly one.
Persistent deployments · Mason, Minesmith, Nest
Mason: conservative or marks-only; preview friendly/enemy detours and never sever the authored friendly route.

Minesmith: use choke/patrol marks and do not replace a useful live mine.

Nest: show eight rays/range and do not replace a useful seed; require team contact in the alpha rule.
Rescue geometry · Switchback
Use only when an endangered carrier/veteran/low-health ally becomes safer and both destinations remain acceptable. Preview the swap and the Core consequence before the player saves it.
Later combat mobility · Mobile flank
Do not bundle this into the signature pass. First make ordinary Aggressive chase honest. Mobile flank can later be an explicit opt-in that uses reload windows and a stable two-step side goal inside the fight leash—never hidden class AI.

Acceptance boundary

Combat proof

Posts no longer cancel the game

  • A posted Aggressive bot chases at least three tiles when useful, attacks, and returns after contact.
  • Hold still ignores bait but closes on a carrier/counter-range threat within five.
  • Observe never voluntarily fights.
  • Core pickup, urgent recovery, phase change and abort pre-empt without orphaning an episode.
  • No orbit, repeated reversal, wall pin or chase beyond the fixed leash.
Patrol proof

Interruptions preserve route progress

  • A patrolling Aggressive bot fights from a mid-route contact and resumes the same next waypoint afterward.
  • Observe patrol does not voluntarily leave its route to fight.
  • A point/segment skill fires only while naturally passing; illegal use continues the patrol that tick.
  • A rooted signature resumes the same cursor after completion or cancellation.
  • Revisiting a deployment mark does not replace a healthy useful deployment.
Custody proof

The Core keeps its pickup-phase policy

  • Pick up Core A in P1, advance the squad to P2, and prove its carrier and escorts still use P1 route and custody-safe skill policy.
  • Drop Core A to fight in P2, then let the same or another friendly bot pick it up; prove P1 custody resumes from useful forward route progress without backtracking.
  • Handoff and Relay Toss move Core A to a receiver without rebinding it to P2.
  • Damage the carrier and an attached escort below the ordinary recovery threshold; both stay on task and neither paths to a healing tile. After custody ends, ordinary Recovery becomes eligible again.
  • Observe carrier runs; Hold/Aggressive may return fire but never reverse or chase off-route. Carrier-safe support signatures remain usable; Kestrel never Dashes carrying.
  • Death-drop, enemy pickup and temporary loss make P1 provenance dormant rather than deleting it; friendly recapture restores it. Banking Core A clears it, and a newly born Core B binds afresh.
  • No carrier identity, escort target, fight episode or route cursor survives after that Core's lifecycle actually resolves.
Authoring proof

Duplicate bots are genuinely distinct

  • Two Masons/Relays/etc. keep separate policy, marks, targets and migration state.
  • Selecting one bot exposes only its own signature plan.
  • Adding guidance is map-first and does not expose generated JSON.
  • Rules-derived range, footprint, timing and collateral previews agree with the engine.
  • Practice explains both use and decline.
Executor proof

One visible priority remains

  • Every Preserve-by-default skill continues until authoritative completion/cancel.
  • A rejected skill falls through in the same tick.
  • Longshot cannot Rail off station; Kestrel cannot Dash carrying.
  • Relay Toss remains custody, not generic combat automation.
  • Every emitted action keeps a non-empty reason.
Play review

Evidence, not a balance claim

  • One fixed micro-scene per signature proves mechanics and UX intent.
  • One standard six-cell exhibit uses a real authored sheet with the combined pass.
  • Review the motivating post-chase, patrol-resumption and signature-starvation scenes first.
  • Do not alter damage/range/cooldowns or run a balance battery in this goal.
  • Mobile flank stays deferred until ordinary chase is satisfying.

Bottom line

The game now asks players to position bots with Posts, so Posts must be compatible with combat—not a reason combat quietly stops. Fix that lifecycle first. Then the signature inventory is the right next investment: it turns sixteen existing mechanics from unreliable automation into readable tactical tools without adding a second priority system or more classes.
