Recommendation

Yes: run one more refinement round before adding more Tactics.

Its first task is combat lifecycle, not signature UI. The current map-first editor makes Posts the normal spatial assignment. Hold now correctly starts at its post, pursues within a bounded defence episode, then returns. Aggressive does not retain that episode after it takes the first step away from a post. That contradicts the editor promise “Chase, commit, finish kills.”

After that repair, implement the inventory's slot-local signature plans, commitment, priority and safe opportunity predicates as one staged goal. Do not tune damage, ranges or cooldowns in this round. First make the existing mechanics dependable and understandable.

The corrected posts rule

Replace “authored posts always win” with this:

A post owns staging, resting facing, the fight's origin and the return destination. An active fight episode temporarily owns combat movement inside the posture's leash. Phase changes, Core custody, urgent recovery and signature commitment can pre-empt it normally.

Reach postThe exact tile and facing establish the bot's authored ground.
Contact qualifiesThe selected posture decides whether and how a fight may start.
Latch episodeThe activation tile becomes a fixed chase-leash origin. Leaving ±1 of the post does not cancel it.
Fight or pre-emptUse skills, shoot and pursue within the leash. Custody, recovery and phase lifecycle remain higher.
Return homeWhen contact ends, the ordinary post Move resumes and restores its facing.
Observe → never starts a voluntary fight; post/facing remain authoritative. Hold → starts on station; latches; pursues only a carrier or a gun it cannot answer; leash 5. Aggressive→ starts on station; latches; pursues useful targets; leash 6; finishes or loses contact, then returns. Entry gate = empty-handed AND enemy-nearby AND on-station Exit gate = carrying-core OR enemy-no-longer-nearby Movement = existing Fight action + fixed activation anchor + chaseLimit This uses the existing Instruction lifecycle and fight anchor. It is not a new state machine.

Patrol remains different: its route is the moving ground. “On the way” behavior still governs ingress. The change is specifically that an arrived post does not pull an active Aggressive fighter home every second tick.

How the sixteen signatures enter the pass

Transit & custody · Kestrel, Relay
Kestrel: Dash only empty-handed along the active route/marked segment with ≥2 safe forward tiles. Never auto-Dash from a fixed post.

Relay: keep Toss inside custody; require useful forward progress and a legal receiver; failed Toss never prevents movement/banking.
Protect & sustain · Palisade, Patchbay
Palisade: screen a real hostile lane protecting self, carrier or ally; respect authored facing; do not replace a useful active screen.

Patchbay: prefer carrier/veteran/lowest health, require a plausible safe channel, then preserve the authoritative channel.
Control · Towline, Hush, Repulsor, Sunder
Towline: beneficial clear pull, carrier first; preview destination and interceptors.

Hush: suppress actual hostile signature value, not generic proximity.

Repulsor: preview every push and protect allied posts/Core direction.

Sunder: paint only when allies can plausibly spend the enhanced hits.
Ranged setup · Longshot, Mortar
Longshot: require the firing post/area, team contact and a clear line; preserve the rooted lock.

Mortar: require occupied/recently confirmed value and zero allies in the cross; render friendly fire and tell.
Information & concealment · Lantern, Veil
Lantern: for alpha, make Flare honestly anti-smoke; Smart fires only where smoke blocks a relevant allied sightline.

Veil: cover a carrier/crossing or break an enemy lane without cutting a critical friendly one.
Persistent deployments · Mason, Minesmith, Nest
Mason: conservative or marks-only; preview friendly/enemy detours and never sever the authored friendly route.

Minesmith: use choke/patrol marks and do not replace a useful live mine.

Nest: show eight rays/range and do not replace a useful seed; require team contact in the alpha rule.
Rescue geometry · Switchback
Use only when an endangered carrier/veteran/low-health ally becomes safer and both destinations remain acceptable. Preview the swap and the Core consequence before the player saves it.
Later combat mobility · Mobile flank
Do not bundle this into the signature pass. First make ordinary Aggressive chase honest. Mobile flank can later be an explicit opt-in that uses reload windows and a stable two-step side goal inside the fight leash—never hidden class AI.

Acceptance boundary

Combat proof

Posts no longer cancel the game

  • A posted Aggressive bot chases at least three tiles when useful, attacks, and returns after contact.
  • Hold still ignores bait but closes on a carrier/counter-range threat within five.
  • Observe never voluntarily fights.
  • Core pickup, urgent recovery, phase change and abort pre-empt without orphaning an episode.
  • No orbit, repeated reversal, wall pin or chase beyond the fixed leash.
Authoring proof

Duplicate bots are genuinely distinct

  • Two Masons/Relays/etc. keep separate policy, marks, targets and migration state.
  • Selecting one bot exposes only its own signature plan.
  • Adding guidance is map-first and does not expose generated JSON.
  • Rules-derived range, footprint, timing and collateral previews agree with the engine.
  • Practice explains both use and decline.
Executor proof

One visible priority remains

  • Every Preserve-by-default skill continues until authoritative completion/cancel.
  • A rejected skill falls through in the same tick.
  • Longshot cannot Rail off station; Kestrel cannot Dash carrying.
  • Relay Toss remains custody, not generic combat automation.
  • Every emitted action keeps a non-empty reason.
Play review

Evidence, not a balance claim

  • One fixed micro-scene per signature proves mechanics and UX intent.
  • One standard six-cell exhibit uses a real authored sheet with the combined pass.
  • Review the motivating post-chase and signature-starvation scenes first.
  • Do not alter damage/range/cooldowns or run a balance battery in this goal.
  • Mobile flank stays deferred until ordinary chase is satisfying.

Bottom line

The game now asks players to position bots with Posts, so Posts must be compatible with combat—not a reason combat quietly stops. Fix that lifecycle first. Then the signature inventory is the right next investment: it turns sixteen existing mechanics from unreliable automation into readable tactical tools without adding a second priority system or more classes.
