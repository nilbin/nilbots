The promoted chassis cadences, measured travel impact, carrier timing, and the bounded Mobile flank follow-up.

Arc Relay movement-rate design

Status: Tapline-11 promoted mechanical baseline; combat mobility remains a reviewed follow-up.

#Verdict

Tapline-11 should make swift, standard, and deliberate honest movement rates instead of three unrelated handling exceptions:

chassis public move opportunities rate facing while moving
swift 1 of every 1 tick 1.00 Combat Strafe
standard 4 of every 5 ticks 0.80 Combat Strafe
deliberate 2 of every 3 ticks 0.67 Combat Strafe

Every accepted move is still one adjacent tile. Closed opportunities do not bank, so waiting cannot create a burst. Stable unit ID staggers closed ticks; an equal-rate squad does not visibly stop in lockstep. The rate is part of the canonical rules contract, SDK, replay wire, manifest, CLI output, and hosted playlist—not an unexplained executor heuristic.

Combat Strafe is one control model for every chassis: forward and lateral travel can preserve aim, while rearward travel turns the body. Tapline-10 and older profiles retain their exact historic facing models and universal 1/1 travel.

The renderer still receives ordinary one-tile relocations. A missed movement opportunity is a stationary tick; it does not stretch one tile across several ticks, so per-tile animation remains brisk.

#600-tick reachability

Tapline's enemy Well is 31 steps from the home reactor by the direct route and 41 through Centre. Using the latest opening tick (56), the 46-tick source-local recharge, an empty outbound trip between banks, and three enemy-Well Cores (each worth 4 charge), conservative no-combat destruction times are:

repeated enemy-Well route swift standard deliberate
direct, 31 steps 334 382 429
via Centre, 41 steps 394 469 549
deliberate outer route, 58 steps 520 637 752

So every chassis can still end regulation inside 600 on both practical routes. The 58-step experiment is a viable three-bank plan only for swift travel; it is not a standard or deliberate solo win plan. Parallel Cores, Arc Toss, Dash, combat, denial, and traffic can change real outcomes. This is a reachability proof, not a balance verdict.

#Speed-aware combat movement

Do not make fast bots circle automatically. Rear hits deal 2x damage, while swift classes use the short gun's two-tick firing interval. A baseline rule of “shoot whenever ready, move only during reload” usually grants only one step before the next shot; arbitrary shot delay, on the other hand, risks making 2x rear damage the dominant autopilot. Both behaviors would hide a strategically important decision.

A later explicit phase posture named Mobile flank is mechanically coherent:

  • the player opts a Group into it for one Tactic phase;
  • the phase's zone, route corridor, or posts are the hard movement leash;
  • choose a stable left/right flank side when contact begins and keep it for the episode;
  • allow at most two displacement steps from the episode anchor;
  • every step must strictly improve a reachable rear-angle firing tile or return progress—never orbit, reverse, kite, or backtrack;
  • authored posts/routes, Core custody, recovery, and higher-priority Instructions always win;
  • stop repositioning when the gun is ready, the target is lost, the bot gains a Core, the phase changes, or the leash would be crossed;
  • surface repositioning, shot ready, no improving tile, and returning in Practice diagnostics.

An advanced preset could later permit sacrificing exactly one ready short-gun shot to seek the second flank step, but it must be explicit and visually disclosed. Slower bodies may eventually get a separate Screen advance preset that advances behind cover or holds between a protected ally and a threat; it should not be bundled into Mobile flank.

The current executor can support this without a second scheduler: the Tactics compiler can emit ordinary bounded Fight/Move Instructions under the existing top-to-bottom priority and phase lifecycle. What is missing is a reviewed player-facing preset plus a small stable-side/episode fact in the existing fight continuation. That should be implemented only after the simple chassis rates have been played, because it is a tactical product choice rather than a movement legality requirement.

#Acceptance boundary

  • Implement now: canonical public cadence, unified Combat Strafe, immutable Tapline-11 and hosted playlist v28, historical compatibility, focused route and legality proofs.
  • Defer: Mobile flank, Screen advance, class-specific combat AI, or any hidden kiting/circling policy.
  • Balance follow-up: compare carrier class share, successful 4-charge returns, travel deaths, and decisive-tick distributions before tuning these rates.
