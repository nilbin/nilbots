WHAT IS MISSING

Three safe additions worth hearing.

These are suggestions, not runtime changes. Each is backed by an explicit public replay fact.

HIGH CONFIDENCE10

Ally handoff

Handoffs are the most specifically “relay” play in Arc Relay, yet core-handed-off is silent. A compact two-part transfer sound rewards authored coordination without reading as a score.

HIGH CONFIDENCE11

Projectile deflection

The timeline already distinguishes a deflection, and replay v3 carries projectile-deflected. Its bright glancing voice would make successful defensive geometry legible without adding more impact noise.

USE SPARINGLY12

Unit arrival

Destruction already speaks; the opposite beat does not. One quiet online cue can cover respawn and fabrication from their explicit lifecycle events, with a low voice cap so team wipes do not become a startup chorus.
