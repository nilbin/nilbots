# Veterancy split-crown feather fidelity brief

This task generates one reusable ornament module for the owner-approved rank 1–5 split diadem. It does not generate a bot, a complete crown, a paired set, or any gameplay object.

## Must preserve

- One slim, rigid, layered mechanical feather with a clipped tip and large readable facets.
- A shallow profile and compact graphite root that can mount repeatedly on a small rear arc.
- Matte, desaturated aged warm brass/bronze rather than shiny plastic or saturated toy color.
- Graphite only in recessed backing, root, and seams; it must not make the gameplay silhouette dark.
- A narrow, dim warm-gold channel that remains neutral to teal and orange team identity.
- One connected object suitable for mirrored renderer instances.

## Runtime composition intent

- Rank 1 starts with two separated rear-quarter arcs and no feathers.
- Ranks 2–4 add one feather to each side per rank.
- Rank 5 completes four feathers per side, forming an open moose-antler/crown silhouette.

This reusable-feather renderer composition was superseded by five complete Meshy rank
models after gameplay-scale review. The brief remains as historical provider provenance.
- Both halves rotate with bot yaw, stay world-up, and remain independent of recoil and body lean.
- The full-height center corridor remains empty at every rank so Core pickup, carry, handoff, drop, bank, and Arc Toss animation can pass through without ornament state changes.

## Reject

- A whole crown, bilateral pair, wing, organic antler, weapon, bot body, floor, or pedestal.
- Broad fan geometry, long central bridge, center spike, or large root plate.
- Teal/cyan or saturated orange team-color panels.
- Glossy plastic, chrome, lacquer, white nose pieces, human eyes, or baked lighting and cast shadows.
