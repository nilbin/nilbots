## Exact prompt

> Use case: stylized-concept
>
> Asset type: Nilbots game prop concept sheet intended for later Meshy 7 multi-image-to-3D input
>
> Input images: Image 1 is the approved Lantern robot family and material reference only. Image 2 is the existing Survey Flare mechanics storyboard; use only the middle row to understand flight-to-landed function, not its generic spherical flare design.
>
> Primary request: design one compact physical Survey Flare device launched by Lantern. The exact same rigid object must read as a weighted survey dart while horizontal in flight and as a planted compact survey beacon when the renderer rotates it upright after landing. No transformation, opening, telescoping, detached, or moving pieces.
>
> Subject: a short thick faceted survey dart with a strong broad circular optical iris at the nose, a weighted tapered rear, and exactly three flush longitudinal stabilizer ribs integrated into the body. The iris and outer silhouette must remain identifiable around 18–30 gameplay pixels. Mechanical design belongs unmistakably to the approved Lantern family without copying the entire robot.
>
> Style/medium: premium stylized 3D hard-surface game asset concept, compact chunky proportions, restrained worn industrial finish, high roughness matte/satin PBR, broad readable masses, low micro-detail, suitable for a small GLB below roughly 2,000 triangles after optimization.
>
> Composition/framing: one clean 3-by-2 turnaround sheet, six equal panels, exactly one isolated object per panel at identical scale: strict front, strict right side with nose facing right/+X, strict rear, strict top with nose facing right/+X, front-right three-quarter, and landed gameplay-oblique with the same object rotated vertically onto its weighted rear. Camera-consistent orthographic views, generous padding, no cropping. No words, labels, arrows, dimensions, borders, or typography.
>
> Scene/backdrop: uniform warm light-gray studio background across every panel, no floor grid, no environment, no cast shadow.
>
> Lighting/mood: soft diffuse neutral studio light that shows geometry without baked directional highlights, bloom, glow spill, or dramatic reflections.
>
> Color palette: muted heather/eggplant-purple ceramic armor, aged warm ivory scanner casing, tarnished dull brass iris rings and rib hardware, charcoal graphite recesses. One tiny neutral pale-lilac lens light only. No teal/cyan and no orange because those are team colors.
>
> Materials/textures: matte ceramic and aged ivory, dull brushed brass only on small hardware, charcoal mechanisms, restrained edge wear and recess grime; nothing glossy, plastic, chrome, toy-like, or black-dark.
>
> Constraints: one watertight connected object; +X forward is the iris/nose; no team-color paint; no baked scan ring, beam, field, trail, particles, smoke, floor glow, shadow, text, logo, decals, or gameplay UI. No tripod, legs, feet, deployed arms, floating rings, antenna wire, satellite dish, gun barrel, missile fins, lamp handle, Core-like sphere, mine-like puck, turret, humanoid features, or separate pieces. It must not resemble a weapon, grenade, mine, Core, sentry, or objective beacon. Preserve identical proportions and exact hardware placement across all six views. Make the physical object larger in frame than the faint energy effect in Image 2; do not reproduce the effect.
>
> Avoid: over-saturated purple, near-black body, white plastic, glossy toy surfaces, generic capsule, smooth pill, cheap sci-fi greebles, asymmetry that drifts across views, perspective inconsistency, duplicate objects within a panel, watermark.
