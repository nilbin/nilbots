What this round replaces

Both read as assets from a different family; the new concepts preserve their gameplay identities while adopting their parent classes’ materials and proportions.

Exact prompts, ordered references, dimensions and SHA-256 hashes are stored beside this sheet in PROMPTS.md and manifest.json. The previous Sentinel sheet is retained as provenance. No game files, runtime manifests, or provider tasks changed; the paid-call gate remains closed.
