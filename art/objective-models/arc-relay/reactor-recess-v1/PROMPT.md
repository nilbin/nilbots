# Team Reactor recess concept prompt

```text
Use case: stylized game-environment concept art for owner approval before Meshy 7 multi-image-to-3D.

Create a clean 3-by-2 six-view concept sheet of exactly ONE rigid TEAM REACTOR FLOOR SOCKET for the dark sci-fi arena game Nilbots. This must be a completely new design: a very flat recessed reactor pit, maintenance hatch, or hole-in-the-ground insert—not a raised machine, station, gate, bot, turret, or building.

GEOMETRY AND GAMEPLAY:
- One connected, watertight, broad octagonal or subtly tri-lobed floor insert.
- About 1.4–1.5 game tiles across, but extremely low: the physical rim rises no more than roughly 6–8% of its diameter.
- At least 85% of the visual mass appears at or below the arena floor plane.
- A large unmistakable dark central aperture/recess makes it read as a reactor hole from a zoomed-out top-oblique game camera.
- The central hole is open and uncluttered; no glowing orb, core sphere, pillar, machinery tower, bridge, canopy, or floor-standing parts.
- Use a wide beveled outer rim with three large, shallow, top-facing indicator bays spaced clearly around it. These bays remain empty and unlit so the renderer can place the exact 0–3 reactor charge/integrity state.
- Reserve a simple broad top-facing segment of the rim for a renderer-owned team crest. Do not bake the crest.
- The perimeter must remain visually clean enough that hovering bots occupying nearby/overlapping tiles are never hidden.
- Strong silhouette at 18–30 gameplay pixels: one bright outer rim, one dark central hole, three broad indicator bays. Avoid tiny greebles and busy seams.

MATERIAL AND COLOR:
- High-roughness matte/satin environment materials, deliberately less shiny than a robot.
- Medium-light warm gray and aged ivory ceramic on the broad rim, dull nickel edge details, sparse muted burgundy/oxblood clamps, deep graphite inside the aperture.
- Bright enough to read in a dark arena but restrained and mature, not white, black, glossy, plastic, chrome, wet, neon, or toy-like.
- Absolutely no teal, cyan, blue-green, or orange; those are team-owned renderer colors.
- No baked glow, bloom, emission, light beams, ownership ring, progress ring, pips, team paint, numbers, text, labels, logos, core counter, damage, smoke, or fire.

SHEET AND CAMERAS:
- Six equal panels, identical object proportions and hardware in every panel, consistent scale and orientation, generous padding, nothing cropped.
- Top row: strict front orthographic at floor level showing how thin the rim is; strict right orthographic; strict rear orthographic.
- Bottom row: strict top orthographic; front-right three-quarter; pinned 52-degree top-oblique gameplay view.
- In side views, show the insert as a very thin rim with a shallow bowl/cavity extending below the nominal floor line; do not include a separate slab or room-sized floor plane.
- Uniform neutral medium-light warm gray seamless studio background in every panel, soft diffuse neutral lighting, no cast shadow, no vignette, no dramatic reflections.
- No titles, captions, arrows, dimensions, panel labels, borders, watermark, bots, UI, arena scenery, or extra objects.

Meshy suitability: preserve rigid geometry exactly across all six views; no moving, floating, detached, opening, transparent, or animated parts; clean readable hard-surface forms appropriate for an optimized GLB.
```
