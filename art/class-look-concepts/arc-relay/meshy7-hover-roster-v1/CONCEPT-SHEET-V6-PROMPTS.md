# Accepted v6 silhouette and dark-arena prompts

The final v6 sheet was created by applying these three built-in image edits in
order to `concept-sheet-v5.png`. The first two results were inputs to later
edits and are not canonical.

## 1. Separate Mortar, Hush, Longshot, and Nest

Use case: precise-object-edit

Asset type: targeted silhouette-separation pass on the 4-by-4 Nilbots Arc Relay
gameplay roster

Input images: Image 1 is the edit target and canonical no-eyes, no-guns,
no-shared-nose v5 roster.

Primary request: change only MORTAR, HUSH, LONGSHOT, and NEST so their
silhouettes remain unmistakably different at roughly 48 visible pixels.
Preserve their gameplay meanings and the established family rendering.

MORTAR — row 2, column 2:

Replace the circular puck outer silhouette with a low, stout, straight-sided
asymmetric hexagonal launch platform: six clearly faceted armor edges, broad
heavy rear shoulders, a narrower blunt front, and three chunky integrated
buttress blocks. Preserve forest-olive armor, soot-black mechanisms, and the
copper/gold hardware accent. Keep one large closed segmented hexagonal Falling
Star launch iris recessed into the upper rear-left top plane, with a thick
polygonal recoil collar. The iris must read as a closed upward launch hatch,
not a circular field disc and not a gun. Absolutely no round outer perimeter,
cannon, tube, barrel, turret, missile, legs, or stabilizer feet.

HUSH — row 2, column 4:

Replace the circular puck outer silhouette with a compact four-lobed absorber
form: one low flattened indigo-black central ovoid with four broad, short,
attached absorber-fin lobes at front, rear, left, and right, separated by four
deep silhouette notches. Keep it wider than tall but not a circle, wheel, disc,
or ring. Preserve the recessed muted-lavender Null Field core in the center and
brushed-silver structure, but simplify the core to a dark low oval/rounded
diamond rather than a target-like set of concentric circles. All lobes stay
physically attached; no floating ring and no eye/face cue.

LONGSHOT — row 3, column 3:

Increase the chassis width by roughly 35–45 percent while preserving its
exceptional length, East/right facing, and flat chisel prow. Target a clear
length-to-width ratio around 4.5:1 instead of a hair-thin needle. Broaden the
glacier-blue side armor and dark-navy lower structure symmetrically around the
existing body-length silver rail. Keep three large staged rail charge cells
along the spine, spaced for readability. It should read as a substantial long
rail barge rather than a rifle, gun, spear, or aircraft. Preserve the gunless
flat chisel termination and almost invisible flush beam seam; no muzzle,
barrel, scope, ammunition, or white nose module.

NEST — row 4, column 4:

Replace the rounded compact carrier silhouette with a distinctly wide, angular
twin-rack carrier approximately 1.5 times wider than deep. Build two thick
pine-green/umber side rails separated by a broad dark central deployment lane
and a deep rear-center notch/cradle, giving the top silhouette a chunky open H
or squared horseshoe character rather than a puck or rounded rectangle.
Preserve exactly six large closed Sentinel Seed cells, three securely embedded
on the left rack and three on the right rack, with aubergine/dark caps and
strong separation at 48 pixels. Keep all cells attached and closed. No visible
sentry, loose pod, missile cue, organic eggs, wings, or gun.

Four-way separation requirement at thumbnail scale:

- MORTAR = faceted asymmetric hexagon with one closed offset launch iris.
- HUSH = dark compact four-lobed absorber with deep notches.
- LONGSHOT = long but noticeably thicker rail barge.
- NEST = very wide open twin-rack/H-shaped six-cell carrier.

Their top silhouette outlines must not overlap conceptually or collapse into
the same low puck.

Critical invariants: change only those four named cells. Do not alter KESTREL,
PALISADE, TOWLINE, PATCHBAY, LANTERN, MINESMITH, RELAY, SWITCHBACK, MASON,
SUNDER, REPULSOR, or VEIL. Preserve the exact 4-by-4 grid, elevated
near-orthographic 58-degree gameplay camera, East/right facing, scale
consistency, labels, spelling, cell order, no-eye direction, no-gun direction,
no universal white/pearl nose module, no visible shared charge plates, no fixed
glow, neutral background, dividers, and complete uncropped framing. Preserve
only these labels:

Row 1: KESTREL, PALISADE, TOWLINE, PATCHBAY

Row 2: LANTERN, MORTAR, MINESMITH, HUSH

Row 3: RELAY, SWITCHBACK, LONGSHOT, MASON

Row 4: SUNDER, REPULSOR, VEIL, NEST

Team color and attack invariants: teal and orange remain renderer-owned
opposing team colors. No baked team glow. Every bot's ordinary beam remains an
almost invisible hairline seam in its own leading-edge armor; do not add a
contrasting nose or weapon hardware.

Avoid: circular Mortar; circular Hush; rounded compact Nest; hair-thin Longshot;
shared puck silhouette; white nose; pearl prow cap; shared bumper; charge
plates; gun; barrel; muzzle; bore; nozzle; rifle; missile; face; eye motif;
active charge; beam; projectile; extra object; changed label; extra text; logo;
watermark; scenery; cast shadow; contact shadow.

## 2. Lift authored values for the dark arena

Use case: precise-object-edit

Asset type: material-value readability pass on the 4-by-4 Nilbots Arc Relay
gameplay roster for a predominantly dark arena

Input images: Image 1 is the edit target. Its geometry and labels are final for
this pass.

Primary request: change only the authored hull paint/material values so every
bot remains readable against a dark game arena. Do not brighten the background,
exposure, studio lighting, or shadows. Instead, make the actual armor pigments
and metal materials lighter and more value-separated.

Roster-wide value rules:

- Raise the dominant visible armor midtones by roughly 15–25 percent while
  preserving every class's palette family.
- Give every bot at least one large light or upper-mid-value top-plane armor
  region and one distinct medium-value region. Keep the value blocks broad
  enough to survive at 48 visible pixels.
- Limit near-black/soot/basalt material to recessed mechanisms, underside gaps,
  narrow seams, and at most about 20 percent of each visible bot. Do not let
  broad outer silhouette panels remain near-black.
- Preserve strong material contrast and saturation without turning the bots
  pastel, chalky, washed out, glowing, or overexposed.
- Edge readability must come from fixed pigment/value and clean armor
  boundaries, not baked rim light, outline, bloom, halo, or environmental
  reflection.
- Keep all emissive surfaces off. Teal and orange remain renderer-owned
  opposing team colors and must not appear as baked glow.

Specific dark-bot corrections:

- MORTAR: shift the large forest-olive hexagonal armor to a lighter moss/khaki
  olive, brighten copper hardware to warm brass, and keep charcoal only in the
  closed launch iris, seams, and small recesses. Preserve its faceted asymmetric
  hexagon and offset hatch.
- HUSH: it must retain a stealth/suppression identity without becoming a black
  hole. Shift the four large lobes from near-black to clearly visible medium
  indigo/plum composite, use broad muted-lavender upper armor panels on each
  lobe, and add restrained brushed-silver structural borders. Keep only the
  deep notches and recessed center core near-black. Preserve the exact
  four-lobed silhouette; no glow and no concentric target.
- NEST: lighten the wide twin side racks to medium pine/sage green, brighten
  structural rails to warm tan/umber, and make all six pod caps medium
  aubergine/plum with thin brass retaining rings. Keep only the open central
  deployment lane and deep rear cradle dark. Preserve the wide H/twin-rack
  silhouette and exactly six cells.
- REPULSOR: lift the broad burgundy armor toward medium brick-red, increase
  rose-gold radial structure, and confine basalt-black to narrow coil gaps.
- VEIL: lift the broad crescent armor to a clear medium emerald/sage value and
  use larger sand-colored panels; keep charcoal only between plates and around
  the two sealed canister recesses.
- SWITCHBACK: preserve the white/black twin-lobe concept, but lift the black
  lobe to medium charcoal graphite with broad dark-silver top planes so it
  remains visible in darkness; keep the white lobe off-white rather than pure
  white.
- LANTERN: retain ultraviolet purple/ivory/gold, but lift the lower purple shell
  to a medium plum so its outer silhouette does not disappear.
- SUNDER: lift the darkest graphite wedge surfaces to medium charcoal while
  retaining magenta-plum and ivory hierarchy.
- PALISADE and LONGSHOT: preserve cobalt and glacier-blue identity but lift the
  deepest navy surfaces enough to remain readable.
- KESTREL, TOWLINE, PATCHBAY, MINESMITH, RELAY, and MASON are already
  relatively bright; preserve their palettes and only prevent unnecessary
  near-black outer panels.

Critical invariants: color/value edit only. Preserve every silhouette,
proportion, geometry, class-signature apparatus, camera angle, 4-by-4 grid,
scale, label, spelling, cell order, no-eye rule, no-gun rule, no universal
white/pearl nose, no shared charge plates, invisible flush beam seams, neutral
light-gray background, white dividers, and full uncropped framing. Preserve
only these labels:

Row 1: KESTREL, PALISADE, TOWLINE, PATCHBAY

Row 2: LANTERN, MORTAR, MINESMITH, HUSH

Row 3: RELAY, SWITCHBACK, LONGSHOT, MASON

Row 4: SUNDER, REPULSOR, VEIL, NEST

Avoid: geometry redesign; altered silhouette; dark outer mass; near-black
dominant armor; black-on-black value grouping; global exposure change; brighter
background; baked rim light; outline; glow; bloom; teal/cyan/turquoise/aqua
emission; orange/amber emission; underbody glow; face; eye motif; white nose;
gun; muzzle; barrel; active charge; beam; projectile; extra object; changed
label; extra text; logo; watermark; scenery; cast shadow; contact shadow.

## 3. Brighten Palisade blue

Use case: precise-object-edit

Asset type: targeted Palisade color tuning on the brightened 4-by-4 Nilbots
roster

Input images: Image 1 is the edit target and geometry/value-approved roster.

Primary request: change only PALISADE in row 1, column 2, and change only its
blue armor pigment. Move its current pale, slightly washed blue one modest step
brighter and substantially more saturated into a clean vivid
royal-cobalt/cornflower blue. The dominant blue must remain high-value and
clearly readable against a dark arena, but feel strong and deliberate rather
than baby blue, powder blue, pastel, cyan, turquoise, teal, or glowing. Use a
bright saturated royal blue on broad top planes, a slightly deeper cobalt on
side planes, and preserve crisp material boundaries.

Preserve Palisade's exact broad shield-slab geometry, all three attached
antique-gold Prism Wall projector ribs, gold rear structure, dark mechanisms,
proportions, silhouette, camera, label, and cell. Do not introduce pale/white
prow geometry or any new part. Antique gold remains unchanged and should
contrast cleanly with the brighter blue.

Critical invariants: change only Palisade's blue pigment. Do not alter any
other bot, geometry, palette, signature hardware, background, exposure,
lighting, 4-by-4 grid, label, spelling, or cell order. Preserve the no-eyes,
no-guns, no-shared-white-nose, no-visible-charge-module, no-fixed-glow rules.

Avoid: dark navy-dominant Palisade; pale baby blue; pastel blue; cyan; teal;
turquoise; glow; bloom; recolored gold ribs; white nose; gun; face; changed
silhouette; changed label; extra text; logo; watermark.
