# Accepted v3 gameplay-scale edit prompts

The final v3 sheet was created by applying these two built-in image edits in
order to `concept-sheet-v2.png`. The first result was used only as the input to
the targeted correction; it is not canonical.

## 1. Gameplay-oblique no-eye roster

Use case: precise-object-edit

Asset type: owner-review 4-by-4 Nilbots Arc Relay hover-bot roster optimized for
zoomed-out gameplay readability

Input images: Image 1 is the edit target and identity reference. Preserve its
sixteen named class concepts, but revise their presentation and visual
hierarchy for actual gameplay scale.

Primary request: remove the glowing-eye direction completely and rebuild the
sheet as a gameplay-readability concept gate.

Camera and scale: show every bot from the same elevated gameplay-oblique
camera, approximately a pinned 58-degree downward view, facing East/right. Use
near-orthographic projection with very little perspective distortion. Show
much more of each top silhouette than Image 1 while retaining enough
front/right side to identify the prow. Keep every bot large and consistently
scaled within its cell. The design must remain recognizable when each bot is
reduced to roughly 64–96 pixels wide.

Remove eyes: eliminate all paired eye lamps, eye slits, eye pixels, eye
clusters, brows, and facial arrangements from every bot. Do not replace them
with another face or visor. Necessary sensors should be dark, asymmetric,
recessed industrial optics; class-signature optics such as Lantern's survey
lens and Sunder's designator remain functional hardware, not eyes.

Zoomed-out form hierarchy:

- Prioritize one unmistakable outer silhouette, one dominant class-signature
  shape, and two or three large value/color blocks per bot.
- Make every named signature apparatus about 15–25 percent more visually
  prominent and readable from above without changing what it does.
- Reduce tiny panel seams, vents, bolts, micro-lights, and greebles by roughly
  forty percent. Merge them into fewer, larger armor plates.
- Use broad light-versus-dark material separation and thick negative spaces.
  Keep thin parts attached and chunky.
- Preserve strong silhouette distinctions: Kestrel narrow dart; Palisade tall
  broad slab; Towline horseshoe with winch; Patchbay round service capsule with
  repair boom; Lantern survey eye/halo body; Mortar low puck with one tube;
  Minesmith triangular foundry; Hush dark flattened field disc; Relay open U
  catcher; Switchback connected twin lobes; Longshot needle rail; Mason block
  forge with empty bay/forks; Sunder diamond designator; Repulsor radial field
  disc; Veil shallow crescent; Nest wide six-cell carrier.

Shared basic attack at gameplay scale: every bot winds up for one tick and
fires a straight beam. Replace the tiny eye-like prow lamps with one common,
non-facial charge-readiness system: three broad connected armor/capacitor
plates or bars arranged as a clear directional chevron/band across the
top-front prow, occupying roughly 12–18 percent of the visible chassis width.
In this static concept they are neutral pearl-gray and completely unlit. At
runtime these three plates will illuminate sequentially and tint teal or orange
according to team. Keep one small dark circular beam aperture centered at the
extreme forward edge, visually separate from the broad charge plates and
separate from class-signature hardware. No active charge or beam in the image.

Team color and emission: teal and orange are opposing runtime team colors. Do
not bake either color into emissive strips, glow, underlighting, charge plates,
sensors, or lenses. No fixed colored glow at all. Team-owned charge plates
remain neutral/unlit in this concept. Existing orange or green non-emissive
class armor may remain as fixed paint, but must not glow or be confused with
the semantic charge plates.

Grid and text invariants: preserve an exact four-column by four-row grid with
thin white dividers and one bot per cell. Preserve the exact fixed cell order
and render only these uppercase labels, correctly spelled and centered at the
bottom of their cells:

Row 1: KESTREL, PALISADE, TOWLINE, PATCHBAY

Row 2: LANTERN, MORTAR, MINESMITH, HUSH

Row 3: RELAY, SWITCHBACK, LONGSHOT, MASON

Row 4: SUNDER, REPULSOR, VEIL, NEST

Style and backdrop: premium polished stylized hard-surface 3D game concept art,
coherent family language, clean large forms, diffuse studio light, flat
seamless light-neutral gray per cell, complete uncropped silhouettes, generous
padding. Preserve each class's distinctive non-emissive palette and signature
theme from Image 1.

Avoid: any eye motif; humanoid or animal face; visor face; mouth;
teal/cyan/turquoise/aqua glow; orange/amber glow; underbody glow; active charge;
beam; projectile; Core; mine; sentry; wall; flare; smoke; field effect; legs;
wheels; treads; wings; aircraft cockpit; extra objects; duplicated or missing
bot; changed label; extra text; logo; watermark; scenery; cast shadow; contact
shadow.

## 2. Relay, Switchback, and Mason attack-hardware correction

Use case: precise-object-edit

Asset type: targeted gameplay-readability polish on the 4-by-4 Nilbots Arc
Relay roster

Input images: Image 1 is the edit target, the elevated 58-degree
gameplay-oblique no-eyes roster.

Primary request: change only the shared basic-attack charge hardware on RELAY,
SWITCHBACK, and MASON. Preserve every other bot and every other design choice as
closely as possible.

RELAY: preserve the open U-shaped catcher and empty cradle. Remove the three
neutral plates from only the left arm. Place one centered three-segment neutral
pearl-gray charge band across the forward lower yoke at the midpoint of the U,
with one small dark circular beam aperture directly beneath/forward of the
band. Keep both throwing rails visually unchanged and separate.

SWITCHBACK: preserve the connected white/black twin-lobe silhouette and gold
link coils. Add one centered three-segment neutral pearl-gray charge band
spanning only the narrow front edge of the central bridge, with one small dark
circular beam aperture exactly on the centerline immediately forward of it. Do
not put the shared beam on either individual lobe.

MASON: preserve the empty square shaping bay and both chunky forked hardlight
emitters as its signature. Close both fork tips with unlit solid neutral caps so
they cannot read as twin gun barrels. Add one centered three-segment neutral
pearl-gray charge band on the top-front central brow above the empty bay, plus
one small dark circular beam aperture exactly at the central forward edge below
that band. The standard beam must be separate from both hardlight forks.

Critical invariants: do not alter KESTREL, PALISADE, TOWLINE, PATCHBAY, LANTERN,
MORTAR, MINESMITH, HUSH, LONGSHOT, SUNDER, REPULSOR, VEIL, or NEST. Preserve the
exact 4-by-4 grid and elevated near-orthographic 58-degree gameplay camera;
every silhouette, scale, pose, palette, class-signature apparatus, background,
divider, label, spelling, and cell order; simplified large-form detail
hierarchy; no eyes; no glow; no team color baked into charge hardware. Preserve
labels exactly: KESTREL, PALISADE, TOWLINE, PATCHBAY / LANTERN, MORTAR,
MINESMITH, HUSH / RELAY, SWITCHBACK, LONGSHOT, MASON / SUNDER, REPULSOR, VEIL,
NEST.

Avoid: paired eye lights, face, visor face, mouth, active charge,
teal/cyan/turquoise/aqua glow, orange/amber glow, beam, projectile, extra
object, redesign, duplicated or missing bot, changed text, logo, watermark.
