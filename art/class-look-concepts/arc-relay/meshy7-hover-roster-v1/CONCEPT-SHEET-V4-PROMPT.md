# Accepted v4 gunless correction prompt

The final v4 sheet was created by applying this built-in image edit to
`concept-sheet-v3.png`.

Use case: precise-object-edit

Asset type: gunless gameplay-readable 4-by-4 Nilbots Arc Relay hover-bot roster

Input images: Image 1 is the edit target, the elevated 58-degree
gameplay-oblique no-eyes roster.

Primary request: remove every gun-like visual cue while preserving the
zoomed-out silhouettes, class identities, palettes, fixed grid, camera, and
gameplay-readable three-plate wind-up language.

Shared basic beam on all sixteen bots:

- Remove every protruding cylindrical nose, cannon barrel, round muzzle ring,
  bore, gun tube, weapon pod, and snout-like launcher from the standard forward
  beam hardware.
- Keep the three broad neutral pearl-gray top-front charge plates, integrated
  flush into the prow armor and readable at 48–96 pixels.
- Replace the round aperture with one very thin dark horizontal emitter seam
  recessed flush into the extreme leading edge directly beneath the charge
  plates. The seam has no barrel, ring, cavity, nozzle, lens, glow, or
  protrusion. It should read as an architectural armor joint when idle, not a
  weapon.
- Preserve the one-tick wind-up logic: at runtime the three plates can tint
  teal/orange and illuminate sequentially, then the beam originates from the
  flush seam. Show no active tint, charge, or beam here.
- The charge plates must not resemble teeth, a mouth, eyes, or a face. Use
  three broad, low-contrast trapezoidal armor tiles in one clean directional
  band.

Specific signature corrections:

- PATCHBAY: preserve the articulated repair arm, but replace its tubular/bored
  end with a broad flat circular service pad containing a flush dark glass
  projector surface. No nozzle or gun shape.
- MORTAR: replace the elevated open cannon tube entirely with one broad recessed
  dorsal launch well covered by a thick segmented hexagonal iris hatch,
  slightly tilted but mostly integrated into the top armor. The closed/idle
  hatch supports Falling Star without looking like a gun, artillery barrel,
  turret, chimney, or missile tube.
- LONGSHOT: preserve the long needle silhouette and body-length staged rail
  cells, but remove the circular nose muzzle. Terminate the rail in a flat
  tapered chisel prow with a narrow flush emitter seam; no barrel, bore, rifle,
  scope, or ammunition cue.
- VEIL: remove the exposed tubular top launcher. Restore two sealed, closed,
  low-profile oval canister pods recessed symmetrically into the upper rear
  hull, with no open ends, bores, or gun direction. Preserve the crescent
  silhouette.
- MASON: preserve the two capped hardlight forks as chunky shaping prongs, not
  barrels; no holes or bores at their ends.
- LANTERN and SUNDER: preserve their large circular signature optics as flat
  recessed sensor/designator glass, not protruding lenses or guns.
- TOWLINE, MINESMITH, RELAY, NEST, and all other class apparatus remain clearly
  non-weapon industrial equipment.

Zoomed-out hierarchy invariants: preserve the elevated near-orthographic
58-degree gameplay camera, East/right facing, strong top silhouettes, reduced
microdetail, two or three large value blocks, oversized signature apparatus,
and recognizability at roughly 48–96 visible pixels. Do not restore eye lights
or facial cues.

one bot per cell, fixed class order, and only these correctly spelled labels:

Team color and emission: teal and orange remain opposing runtime team colors.
No baked teal/cyan/turquoise/aqua glow, no orange/amber glow, no underbody glow,
and no fixed colored emission. Charge plates remain neutral and unlit.
