# Accepted v5 shared-nose removal prompts

The final v5 sheet was created by applying these two built-in image edits in
order to `concept-sheet-v4.png`. The first result was used only as the input to
the targeted Palisade correction; it is not canonical.

## 1. Remove the universal white nose

Use case: precise-object-edit

Asset type: owner-review 4-by-4 Nilbots Arc Relay hover-bot roster without the
shared white nose module

Input images: Image 1 is the edit target and canonical gunless gameplay-scale
roster.

Primary request: remove the universal white/pearl segmented nose thing
completely from every one of the sixteen bots. Do not preserve, recolor,
resize, reinterpret, or replace that shared module.

For every bot:

- Delete the contrasting white/pearl three-panel prow cap, bumper, wedge, beak,
  band, and segmented charge-plate geometry introduced in the previous pass.
- Rebuild the affected prow as a seamless continuation of that individual
  bot's own existing chassis geometry, materials, and fixed class palette. Flow
  the surrounding class-colored armor naturally to the leading edge.
- Do not give the roster another shared visible nose part. Each prow should
  return to its own shape and value grouping.
- Preserve only one almost invisible hairline dark emitter seam integrated
  flush into an existing front armor joint. It must have no contrasting
  surround, no plate assembly, no glow, no lens, no ring, no muzzle, no
  protrusion, and no readable weapon shape.
- The standard one-tick wind-up will be renderer-owned: a temporary teal/orange
  light sweep across each bot's existing front-edge material mask. Do not
  represent that runtime mask with white, pale, outlined, or segmented geometry
  in this static concept.

Class-palette integration examples:

- KESTREL: continue its orange spine and porcelain/navy hull planes into a
  clean sharp tip; no white segmented beak.
- PALISADE: continue cobalt, gold, and quartz shield armor into the prow; remove
  the small white lower cap as a separate object.
- TOWLINE: continue yellow work armor and black structure around a blunt tug
  prow.
- PATCHBAY: continue seafoam/coral/pearl service-shell contours without a white
  bumper.
- LANTERN: continue purple, ivory, and gold spherical armor into a small
  integrated lower prow.
- MORTAR: continue olive armor and gold iris-rim language into a low flush front
  edge.
- MINESMITH: continue terracotta/mustard/gunmetal triangular armor to the point.
- HUSH: continue indigo-black/lavender absorber armor to a quiet low prow.
- RELAY: continue crimson/brass/cream yoke armor through its centered forward
  bridge.
- SWITCHBACK: preserve its white/black twin-lobe identity, but do not add a
  separate white nose module or centered bumper.
- LONGSHOT: continue glacier-blue/navy/silver chisel-rail planes to the tip.
- MASON: continue ochre/blue-gray forge armor between its two capped shaping
  forks; no white center cap and no white fork-tip caps.
- SUNDER: continue magenta/ivory/graphite diamond planes into its sharp
  directional point without a separate white cap.
- REPULSOR: continue burgundy/rose-gold/basalt radial rim into its forward
  sector.
- VEIL: continue emerald/sand/charcoal crescent armor into the prow.
- NEST: continue pine/umber/aubergine carrier armor into a broad integrated
  front edge.

Critical invariants: preserve the exact 4-by-4 grid, elevated near-orthographic
58-degree gameplay camera, East/right facing, all sixteen class silhouettes and
proportions, zoomed-out large-form hierarchy, signature apparatus, no-eye
direction, no-gun direction, class palettes, labels, spelling, cell order,
background, dividers, and full uncropped framing. Preserve only these labels:

Row 1: KESTREL, PALISADE, TOWLINE, PATCHBAY

Row 2: LANTERN, MORTAR, MINESMITH, HUSH

Row 3: RELAY, SWITCHBACK, LONGSHOT, MASON

Row 4: SUNDER, REPULSOR, VEIL, NEST

Team color and emission: teal and orange are opposing runtime team colors. No
baked teal/cyan/turquoise/aqua glow, no orange/amber glow, no underbody glow, no
fixed colored emission, and no visible team-accent plate in this concept.

Avoid: white segmented nose; pearl prow module; shared bumper; charge plates;
three-panel cap; contrasting beak; teeth; mouth; face; eye motif; visor face;
gun; muzzle; barrel; bore; nozzle; lens; active charge; beam; projectile; extra
object; changed silhouette; missing or duplicated bot; label change; extra
text; logo; watermark; scenery; cast shadow; contact shadow.

## 2. Restore Palisade's three projector ribs

Use case: precise-object-edit

Asset type: targeted Palisade signature restoration on the no-white-nose 4-by-4
Nilbots roster

Input images: Image 1 is the edit target, the gameplay-oblique roster with every
universal white nose module removed.

Primary request: change only PALISADE in row 1, column 2. Preserve its broad
cobalt shield-slab hover body and fully integrated cobalt prow with no
white/pearl nose cap. Add exactly three large, thick, attached antique-gold
Prism Wall projector ribs along the East/right-facing outer shield edge,
positioned above and behind the prow rather than on its nose. Arrange the three
ribs in a bold parallel vertical stack that follows the tall shield edge and
remains unmistakable when the bot is reduced to about 48 pixels. Each rib is a
solid rounded rectangular industrial projector housing integrated into the
armor, with dark inset seams but no glow, lens, aperture, barrel, nozzle,
floating part, or weapon cue. Preserve the massive slow shield-slab silhouette.
Do not add pale or white contrasting prow geometry.

Critical invariants: change only Palisade's three gold edge ribs. Preserve
every other Palisade surface, proportion, camera angle, cobalt/gold palette,
label, and cell. Do not alter any pixel-level design decision on KESTREL,
TOWLINE, PATCHBAY, LANTERN, MORTAR, MINESMITH, HUSH, RELAY, SWITCHBACK,
LONGSHOT, MASON, SUNDER, REPULSOR, VEIL, or NEST. Preserve the exact 4-by-4
grid, 58-degree gameplay-oblique camera, labels, spelling, cell order, no-eyes
rule, no-guns rule, no universal white nose rule, no fixed glow, neutral
background, and dividers.

Avoid: white nose; pearl prow cap; shared bumper; charge plates; gun; barrel;
muzzle; bore; nozzle; floating rib; wall beside the bot; active wall; glow;
beam; face; eye motif; changed label; extra text; logo; watermark.
