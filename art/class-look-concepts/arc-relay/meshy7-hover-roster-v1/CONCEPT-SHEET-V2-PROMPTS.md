# Accepted v2 edit prompts

The final v2 sheet was created by applying these two built-in image edits in
order to `concept-sheet.png`. The intermediate result is not canonical.

## 1. Neutral emission and mechanical eyes

Use case: precise-object-edit

Asset type: revised owner-review 4-by-4 Nilbots Arc Relay hover-bot concept
roster

Input images: Image 1 is the edit target and canonical v1 shared roster sheet.

Primary request: Make only two systematic design changes across all sixteen
bots in Image 1:

1. Remove every baked teal, cyan, turquoise, aqua, orange, and amber glow or
   emissive light. Teal and orange are opposing runtime team colors and must
   not appear as fixed emission. Convert all existing cyan/teal underbody glow,
   light strips, capacitor glow, lens glow, and other luminous accents to
   neutral soft white with a very subtle pale-lavender core, or to unlit neutral
   graphite/pearl material where a light is unnecessary. Orange-painted armor
   panels may remain non-emissive class paint; orange must never glow.
2. Give every one of the sixteen bots an unmistakable but non-human robotic
   eye-light motif on the front/prow: preferably two small paired lamps, short
   slits, square pixels, or a compact asymmetric 2–3-light sensor cluster
   depending on the chassis. All eye lights glow neutral soft white with a
   restrained pale-lavender center. They should make the bot's facing and
   personality readable without becoming a face.

Eye requirements: exactly one eye-light motif per bot, visibly present on
KESTREL, PALISADE, TOWLINE, PATCHBAY, LANTERN, MORTAR, MINESMITH, HUSH, RELAY,
SWITCHBACK, LONGSHOT, MASON, SUNDER, REPULSOR, VEIL, and NEST. Use clearly
mechanical lamps protected by armor brows or recesses. No eyeballs, irises,
pupils, eyelids, eyelashes, human eyes, animal eyes, mouths, noses, expressions,
skull faces, emoji faces, or humanoid heads.

Critical separation: preserve each bot's small forward basic-beam aperture as
a separate piece of hardware from its new eye motif. The beam aperture may be a
dark concentric focusing lens with a tiny neutral-white internal point, but it
must not read as the bot's only eye. Preserve all larger class-signature
hardware unchanged and independently readable.

Invariants: preserve the exact 4-by-4 grid, all sixteen class identities,
silhouettes, proportions, poses, camera angles, apparent scale, panel geometry,
signature apparatus, non-emissive class paint colors, light-neutral
backgrounds, white divider lines, and the exact fixed cell order. Preserve all
labels verbatim and correctly spelled: KESTREL, PALISADE, TOWLINE, PATCHBAY /
LANTERN, MORTAR, MINESMITH, HUSH / RELAY, SWITCHBACK, LONGSHOT, MASON / SUNDER,
REPULSOR, VEIL, NEST. No redesign, no replacement bots, no added objects, no
missing bot, no duplicated bot, no changes to text or typography.

Lighting and output: retain the premium polished stylized hard-surface 3D
concept rendering, even diffuse studio illumination, complete uncropped
silhouettes, and clean review-sheet presentation. Neutral-white eye emission
must be restrained and localized with no bloom obscuring geometry.

Avoid: any teal/cyan/turquoise/aqua emission; any orange/amber emission; colored
underbody glow; team-colored light strips; human or animal facial anatomy; a
single central lens being the only eye motif; weapon fire; beam; projectile;
Core; mine; sentry; wall; flare; smoke; field effect; scenery; watermark; extra
text.

## 2. Targeted eye separation polish

Use case: precise-object-edit

Asset type: targeted polish pass on the revised 4-by-4 Nilbots Arc Relay
hover-bot concept roster

Input images: Image 1 is the edit target, the neutral-emission eye-light
revision.

Primary request: Change only the eye-light placement and clarity on three named
bots while preserving every other pixel-level design decision as closely as
possible.

LANTERN: preserve the large central circular survey optic as signature hardware,
but make it clearly not the only eye. Add one unmistakable pair of small
neutral-white, pale-lavender-core rectangular eye lamps side by side in a
protected lower-front brow immediately below the large optic. Keep all other
small lamps restrained.

MASON: the two forked hardlight emitters are signature hardware and must not
double as eyes. Make the fork tips unlit neutral material. Add one unmistakable
pair of small neutral-white, pale-lavender-core square eye lamps side by side in
a recessed front brow above the empty square shaping bay, visually separate
from both forks and from the basic-beam aperture.

SUNDER: preserve the large central circular designator optic as signature
hardware and do not treat scattered side lamps as its eyes. Add one
unmistakable pair of short neutral-white, pale-lavender-core eye slits side by
side on the East/right-facing prow, in one protected dark recess immediately
below and forward of the central optic. Remove or dim any competing scattered
white lights that make its facing ambiguous.

Invariants: do not alter KESTREL, PALISADE, TOWLINE, PATCHBAY, MORTAR,
MINESMITH, HUSH, RELAY, SWITCHBACK, LONGSHOT, REPULSOR, VEIL, or NEST. Preserve
the exact 4-by-4 grid, class order, labels, spelling, typography, silhouettes,
proportions, poses, camera angles, class paint, signature equipment, neutral
light-gray backgrounds, divider lines, and premium hard-surface rendering.
Preserve neutral soft-white/pale-lavender emission and continue to prohibit
every baked teal, cyan, turquoise, aqua, orange, and amber glow.

Avoid: redesigning any bot; changing labels; adding a face, mouth, nose,
eyeball, iris, pupil, eyelid, eyebrow, human eye, animal eye, weapon fire, beam,
projectile, extra object, scenery, or watermark.
