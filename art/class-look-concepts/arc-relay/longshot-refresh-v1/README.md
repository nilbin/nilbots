# Longshot refresh concept v1

Status: **awaiting owner concept approval**. This is not approved Meshy input,
runtime art, provider-task approval, or spend approval.

## Why refresh the current model

The current V3 Longshot is correctly normalized to a `0.99`-tile maximum span,
but its transverse planform span is only `0.436` tile before runtime look scale.
Most of the other approved V3 chassis occupy roughly `0.75`–`0.99` tile across
their second planform axis. Longshot therefore has much less visible surface at
overview scale even though its longest axis is full size.

The equal-sized fleet review hid that cost by framing every model independently.
In the actual arena, the long rounded perimeter becomes a generic blue-gray
pill/coffin and the recessed dark spine absorbs the small same-value charge
blocks. The minimal team mask marks only the prow seam. Model-local lighting
helps its value but cannot invent silhouette or large-scale value hierarchy.

This first refresh direction keeps Longshot elongated and directional while
replacing the pill perimeter with a blunt stepped prow, armored shoulders, a
split stern, and exactly three raised high-value charge stages. The beam source
is a wide flush prow seam, never a gun or barrel.

## Approval contract

- one legless levitating Longshot chassis;
- broad stepped rail-barge silhouette with meaningful vertical relief;
- exactly three large staged dorsal charge blocks;
- medium-light dusty mineral blue, warm gray ceramic, dull nickel, and medium
  graphite in matte/satin industrial materials;
- no teal/cyan or orange authored team colors;
- no eyes, cockpit, humanoid features, guns, barrels, muzzle, white nose, baked
  beam, hover glow, or separate pieces;
- renderer remains authoritative for team color, standard-beam wind-up, Rail
  Line telegraph, beam, damage, timing, and cancellation.

The six-view sheet is concept evidence only. It is not direct Meshy input: the
generated sheet did not obey the requested `+X` screen-right registration in
every panel. After approval, generate and review separate camera-consistent
front, right, rear, top, and gameplay-oblique files before a provider task.

`longshot-gameplay-top.png` is a deterministic crop of the strict top concept
panel. `longshot-gameplay-top-48px.png` is a deterministic `48 x 48` proof for
thumbnail-scale silhouette judgment; neither derivative is a repaint.
