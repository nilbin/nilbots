Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: owner-review concept sheet for sixteen Arc Relay level-5 APEX bot forms.

- Image 1 is the current owner-approved Meshy 7 fleet in the canonical fixed front-right three-quarter camera. It fixes each class identity, material family, order, orientation, and relative scale.

Primary request:
Design a distinct final evolutionary form for every current chassis: what that exact bot becomes when it reaches its level-5 APEX. This is a genuine full-body structural evolution for each class, not a shared crown, halo, badge, ornament, recolor, or bolt-on kit. Preserve clear descent from the current base chassis while making the apex silhouette immediately different at 40–70 gameplay pixels.

Layout:
One polished shared sheet in an exact 4 columns by 4 rows, in this exact fixed order:
row 1: KESTREL, PALISADE, TOWLINE, PATCHBAY
row 2: LANTERN, MORTAR, MINESMITH, HUSH
row 3: RELAY, SWITCHBACK, LONGSHOT, MASON
row 4: SUNDER, REPULSOR, VEIL, NEST.
Exactly sixteen bots, exactly one per equal dark-neutral graphite cell, consistent camera, lighting, padding, and visual scale. Every bot faces East/right (+X) in the same shallow top-down front-right three-quarter gameplay camera. Add each class name once in a small clean header band, spelled exactly as listed, and no other text.

Global chassis rules:
- Every machine levitates. No humanoids, no legs, no feet, no wheels, no treads, no ground-contact locomotion.
- Remain compact, low-profile, and inside essentially the same one-tile gameplay footprint as the base. No giant wings, tall central towers, tall crests, or neighbor-tile overhangs.
- Keep open visual clearance above the central body for the carried glowing Core and UI; put evolved hardware toward the perimeter, shoulders, sides, front, or rear.
- Every bot needs one obvious integrated forward beam aperture that can visibly charge during the standard wind-up and release a beam. It must be part of the chassis, not a conventional gun, rifle, cannon barrel, turret, or mounted weapon. Show no beam or combat effect in this sheet.
- No human or animal eyes, no face, no glowing eye pair, no mouth or nose.
- Strong simple outer contours and one large signature-readable feature per class. Fine panel detail stays subordinate.
- Matte hard-surface materials, restrained edge wear, layered armor, dark recesses, controlled broad highlights. Nothing glossy, shiny, plastic, toy-like, or excessively busy.
- Read clearly against a dark game arena: use light-to-mid-value hulls, never an almost-black overall bot. Colors are muted and sophisticated, not strongly saturated.
- Preserve each current class palette family and keep all sixteen visually distinct. Teal and orange are semantic team colors: do not use teal or orange as fixed hull paint or fixed glow. Small neutral ivory, pale gold, violet-white, or colorless energy slits are acceptable and can later become runtime team masks.
- No shared crown language and no repeated apex ornament. Each class evolves through its own signature hardware.
