# Arc Relay veterancy modules — Meshy 7 static review

This directory holds art-side owner-review evidence for the five-family
Crowned Mantle kit described by
[`MESHY-7-HANDOFF-PLAN.md`](../veterancy-power-concepts-v1/MESHY-7-HANDOFF-PLAN.md).
No GLB here is a runtime asset.

## Current checkpoint

Gate D failed owner concept-fidelity review on 2026-08-14. Five technically
valid normalized rich families exist, but none is owner-approved for runtime.
The owner found that the level-five composition did not resemble the approved
concept art.

The V2 crown remains salvageable: its five connected petals match the intended
family, but the rejected composition showed it too small or edge-on. The swept
and radial pieces are rejected for this direction because their source sheets
already describe broad armor wedges rather than slim layered crown feathers.
Square mantle and curved rail passed technical checks but remain unreviewed in
isolation after the batch-level rejection.

`correction-v2/` contains the authoritative references, a corrective fidelity
brief, and a new slim-feather 2D candidate. No replacement Meshy call has been
made or authorized.

`correction-v3/` contains the newer simplified proposal requested by the
owner: one universal floating diadem that progresses from a broken halo to a
five-prong crown. This supersedes class-mounted replacement feathers as the
primary review direction while retaining the V2 feather as shape-language
reference. It is conceptual art only and consumed no Meshy credits.

`correction-v4/` tests the level-five proposal with the existing carried-Core
contract and at wider gameplay zoom. It introduces a permanent central
no-geometry pocket: the rear-facing diadem frames, but never intersects or
replaces, the detached objective sphere. It is conceptual art only and consumed
no Meshy credits.

`correction-v5/` supersedes the center-prong geometry as the primary proposal.
It uses separate left and right rear-quarter ornament halves, growing to three
feathers per side while leaving a full-height center corridor empty. A clean
beauty sheet and a translucent clearance diagnostic record the same rank 1–5
design. Core animation work remains separate. No Meshy credits were consumed.

## Boundary

The provider results remain intentionally rich (roughly 52–66 MB and
1.52–1.99 million triangles each), but optimization is irrelevant until the
silhouette passes. The next gate is owner approval of the universal motion-safe
split-diadem concept, including its center corridor, maximum width, and
gameplay-scale crown read. Only then should one shared ornament proof be built
and checked at gameplay scale across representative chassis. Lean derivatives,
runtime integration, veterancy animation, and replay capture remain closed.
