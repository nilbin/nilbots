# Meshy 7 veterancy kit assessment

## Verdict

**Gate D failed owner concept-fidelity review.** The technically valid kit does
not yet reproduce the approved Crowned Mantle identity. The level-five board
made the crown too small or edge-on and placed broad armor wedges flat across
the chassis where the concept calls for a compact crown-led arrangement with
slim layered feathers.

This is a static art-side checkpoint, not final Gate D approval. No runtime
asset was promoted, no game renderer or replay data was changed, and no replay
harness was run.

## Corrective crown decision

The first crown task (`01a001c4-aa9c-7b46-bf18-db6473395f8b`) succeeded
technically but failed the approved silhouette: it was a low bridge and read as
another chassis plate. It is rejected but preserved for provenance.

The corrective V2 task (`01a001e6-49a2-7e18-b5d8-c6b7240d5d60`) preserves one
dominant center petal and two progressively lower swept pairs on one graphite
base. Its raw mesh has one spatial connected component. Normalized dimensions
are `0.220 X × 0.120 Y × 0.038 Z` tile units. It remains legible from the real
gameplay three-quarter direction while presenting only a thin footprint in a
strict overhead diagnostic, which is expected for an upright crest.

Meshy's documented API has no retry endpoint. The signed-in web workspace did
not expose the API-created task for a no-charge gallery retry, so the owner
correction was recorded as one separate 30-credit corrective cap. Balance moved
from 860 to 830.

## Corrective directions

The V2 slim feather returns to the authoritative winged-crown shape language:
a long narrow face, layered stepped vanes, clipped tip, graphite root, and one
restrained amber seam.

The newer owner-requested V3 concept simplifies the entire system further. One
floating diadem is shared by all sixteen bots and progresses from a broken halo
to one, three, and finally five crown prongs. It avoids chassis-mounted
clipping and most per-class fitting. Its air gap and level-five width still
need owner review before any modeling.

The V4 carried-Core sheet keeps that ornament fixed and reserves the same
central spherical clearance at every rank. The open U sits behind the Core
anchor, its prongs fan around the reserved volume, and the current detached
objective sphere remains visually dominant at match zoom. Exact clearance and
health-pip placement still require a runtime proof.

The owner then identified the complete pickup/drop/toss path as the harder
constraint. V5 therefore removes the center prong and bridge entirely. Two
rear-quarter halves grow symmetrically from arcs to three feathers per side,
leaving a full-height empty center notch. Its diagnostic sheet records the
ornament's required no-geometry volume; the clean sheet is the proposed visual.
Core animation authoring remains separate and may route within that corridor.

## Remaining caveats and next gate

- The provider models are rich proofs, not mobile candidates: each is roughly
  52–66 MB and 1.52–1.99 million triangles.
- The rejected board proves that layout and orientation are identity-bearing;
  a technically valid attachment cannot be accepted from an isolated
  turntable alone.
- The full all-sixteen static fit sheet and the eight required rank strips are
  not yet complete.
- Gate E lean work remains closed until a concept-matched Kestrel composition
  passes owner review.
- Runtime promotion, rank animation, glow ownership, replay evidence, and the
  rejected numeric plate replacement remain separate renderer work.

## Recommendation

Review `correction-v5/motion-safe-split-diadem-rank-1-5-clean.png` together with
its `motion-safe-split-diadem-rank-1-5-clearance.png` diagnostic. Do not make
another paid call until the bilateral direction, center corridor, and maximum

real gameplay scale across several differently shaped chassis before any
broader work.
