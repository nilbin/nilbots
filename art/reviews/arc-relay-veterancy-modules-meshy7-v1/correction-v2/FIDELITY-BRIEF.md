# Crowned Mantle corrective fidelity brief

Status: **owner-rejected 3D composition; corrected feather input candidate awaiting review**.

## Canonical identity

- `authoritative-power-directions.png` defines the rank progression and the
  maximum-rank winged-crown silhouette.
- `authoritative-full-roster.png` defines the per-chassis scale, integration,
  material hierarchy, and negative-space treatment.
- These are copies of the unchanged canonical assets under
  `art/reviews/veterancy-power-concepts-v1/`, retained here only so the public
  review gallery is self-contained.

## Intended gameplay read

A veteran bot looks physically elevated and more dangerous through one compact
dorsal command crown and progressively revealed layered mantle feathers. At
level 5 the five-part rhythm is unmistakable at gameplay zoom without reading
as weapons, wings, a rank badge, or a second chassis.

## Required anchors

- one centered, upright crown with one dominant center petal;
- two mirrored inner feathers and two mirrored outer feathers;
- feather roots clustered tightly around the crown or tucked closely into the
  owning chassis silhouette;
- long, narrow, layered feather faces with clipped angular tips;
- visible gaps between the five elements;
- rough aged brass, graphite root/recesses, and restrained warm-amber seams;
- total level-5 outline remains subordinate to the bot and does not claim a
  neighboring tile.

## Forbidden cues

- broad triangular shovels or shields;
- guns, swords, projectile heads, functional wings, or flight affordances;
- flat plates laid across the hull;
- a complete ring, halo, turret, wall, carried Core, or signature telegraph;
- glossy plastic, chrome, team teal, team orange, saturated paint, or baked
  bloom.

## Failure diagnosis

The rejected 3D composition failed before Meshy reconstruction. Its swept and
radial source sheets already described broad armor wedges, and the review
renderer placed those pieces flat and far apart. The five-petal crown mesh is
salvageable, but it was shown too small or edge-on. Texture changes cannot fix
either silhouette problem.

## Corrective gate

`corrected-swept-feather-candidate.png` is a new 2D candidate for one reusable
slim feather. No provider call is authorized by this brief. If the owner
approves it, crop and validate its four views, generate one replacement Meshy
model, then test the existing crown plus four instances of the replacement on
Kestrel before revising any other family.
