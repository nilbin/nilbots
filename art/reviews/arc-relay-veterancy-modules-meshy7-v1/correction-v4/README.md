# Carried-Core clearance concept

Status: **owner review candidate; conceptual art only**.

`core-carry-clearance-concept-v2.png` tests the maximum-rank universal floating
diadem in three conditions: empty at close gameplay framing, carrying one Arc
Core at the same framing, and carrying that Core at a wider match zoom.

The proposed runtime contract is deliberately static:

- the open-U diadem remains behind the carried-Core anchor;
- every rank reserves the same central spherical no-geometry volume;
- crown prongs begin outside that volume and fan around it;
- the Core remains a detached sphere, renders in front, and keeps its tether;
- health pips stay above the combined silhouette rather than crossing it; and
- the brass ornament never adopts a team material or moves when custody changes.

The first generated pass incorrectly surrounded the Core with a thick mechanical
ring. The V2 review output removes that ring and restores the current gameplay
contract: one plain energy sphere plus restrained team-possession glow. The
discarded intermediate was not added to the repository.

This image does not establish exact runtime dimensions. A future 3D proof must
derive its reserved sphere from the current Core radius and carried height, then
test the result with both team colours, health pips, and representative chassis
at real gameplay zoom. No Meshy credits or runtime changes were used here.
